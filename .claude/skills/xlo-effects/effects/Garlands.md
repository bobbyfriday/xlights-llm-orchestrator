## Craft

### Identity and role

Garlands draws rows of hanging swags stacked up the buffer and slides the whole field in
**Direction**. **Type** (0–3) picks the swag's curve (0, the plain scallop, in 94% of
placements, 10 seq / 5 songs; 3 in 5% and 2 in 2%, 1 seq each); **Spacing** (3–100) is the gap
between rows as a share of the buffer (10 in 79%, 10 seq / 5 songs; 100 is one swag; 4 is
dense); **Cycles** is how many passes the field makes in the placement (1.0 in 53%, 10 seq /
5 songs; 4.0 in 43%, 1 seq / 1 song). The eight directions are the four straight ones plus
four native bounces (`Up then Down` 13%, 3 seq / 3 songs; `Left then Right` and `Right then
Left` 5% each, 2 / 2; `Down then Up` 3%). With red + black (53 placements, the top palette)
the swag is a thin red line on black; black is a color in 49% (2 seq / 2 songs) and sparkles
are on in 58% (2 / 2) — the garland twinkles.

Breadth is moderate: 10 seq / 5 songs, 129 placements, and 73 are one Light of Christmas file.
Two identities:

- **The garland quad** (56 placements, 1 seq / 1 song): four Garlands on four layers at once —
  Up, Down, Left, Right — Spacing 10, Type 0, Cycles 4.0, fade 0.25 / 0.25, red + black, 8
  beats, beneath four SingleStrands and a Warp on the mega tree. The 45% of placements with
  three or more partners is this one stack.
- **The garland hit** (3 seq / 3 songs for the plain Up / Spacing 10 / Cycles 1.0 row): 1–2
  beats (33%) on the whole display per model, a star or the trees, re-cut on the beat, alone
  (47% of placements have no partner).

Roles: **feature** (the quad on the hero, the 9-beat swing on a star), **carrier** (the hit,
`Per Model Per Preview` on the house), **envelope** (`Brightness` over a Butterfly or Shader,
2 seq / 2 songs), **wash** (`Layered` over four Waves, 1 seq). Not a bed: 4–8 s is 7% and
nothing is longer.

### Thematic fit

Garlands says *draped*, *swinging*, *festooned*: the Christmas rope light, the bunting. `Up`
is a garland hung from the top and lifting; `Up then Down` on a star is a swing; the quad on
the tree is a woven net. Its palettes are the Christmas pairs (red + black, green + blue,
silver + green) and a pastel triad (#FF80FF #80FFFF #8000FF) on the Kid On Christmas star. For
Halloween the drape reads as cobweb or chain: orange + black at Spacing 10 on the hero is a
hanging net, purple + black `Up then Down` on the characters is a swing, one swag at Spacing
100 in orange per stake is a chain hit on the beat. Sparkles on turn it into dew on the web.

**How to permute.** Rotate the quad: Up + Down in the first chorus, Left + Right in the second,
all four at the peak. Keep Spacing 10 for the net and change the color pair per chorus (red +
black → green + blue → silver + green). Between sections change Cycles with the energy (1.0
in the verse hit, 4.0 in the chorus net) and Spacing with the prop (10 on the tree, 100 per
stake). For a twin, flip it (`Flip Horizontal`, 2 seq / 2 songs) or turn it (`Rotate 180`)
and give it the second color.

### Value curves

None: 0% of placements (0 seq / 0 songs). The motion is Cycles and Direction; the breath is
fade 0.25 / 0.25. `effect_meta` speeds it through `E_TEXTCTRL_Garlands_Cycles` 1–4 by
intensity, which is the one dial the corpus moves. A `TT=Beats` Timing Track curve on Spacing
or Cycles would only work after `xlo finalize` patches the Beats track in; `xlo apply` has no
tracks, and the corpus never does it.

### Prop fit and render style

Mega tree 27% (2 seq / 1 song), matrix 19% (1 seq), whole display 17% (5 seq / 4 songs), canes
12% (1 seq), stars 11% (1 seq), mini trees 8% (3 / 3), roofline 4%, floods 2%, arches 1% (1
seq). In this corpus the garland hangs on the tree, the matrix and the whole house, not the
arch. Default buffer on the tree and matrix (the quad); `Per Model Per Preview` on the whole
display so each prop drapes in yard orientation (2 seq / 2 songs); `Horizontal Per
Model/Strand` on cane and roofline groups so each strand gets its own swag (2 / 2); `Flip
Horizontal` on a star. On our layout: the quad on the hero seed matrices, the hit on `SEM_ALL`
per model, `Up then Down` on the characters, one swag per stake at Spacing 100.
`Horizontal Per Model/Strand` and `Horizontal Stack - Scaled` are not in `KNOWN_STYLES` and
fall back to `Per Model Default` — the per-model result is close enough on stakes.

### Anti-patterns

- **Garlands as a bed.** A drape crawling at Cycles 1.0 for 32 beats is not in the corpus (7%
  reach 4–8 s, none longer). Re-cut it every 8 beats like the quad or hit it for 1–2.
- **One Garlands alone on the hero for a section.** The hero identity is the quad or a drape
  under chases; a lone Up drape is the samey look. Alone is for 1–2 beat hits.
- **Type 3 at Spacing 10 on a matrix.** The tight swag at net density is noise; Type 3 lives
  at Spacing 4 on a star (1 seq). On a matrix keep Type 0.
- **Cycles 4.0 on a 2-beat hit.** Four passes in two beats is a flicker; 4.0 belongs to the
  8-beat quad.
- **The pool's first pick.** `Garlands#9` heads the pick pool with Cycles 4.0 and both fades
  frozen — right for the quad, wrong for a hit; `#5` and `#3` are the free ones. Name the look.
- **Our weave's version.** `effect_meta` cells Garlands as a 1–2 bar chase-family carrier at
  energy band 2–2 with `bounce` → `Left then Right`, which is the hit, not the net: the quad's
  four simultaneous directions are four stack layers, never a synthesized carrier.
