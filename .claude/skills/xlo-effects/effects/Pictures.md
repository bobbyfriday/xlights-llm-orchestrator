## Craft

### Identity and role

An image file drawn on the buffer: a PNG or JPEG still, or an animated GIF played back. The file
(`E_FILEPICKER_Pictures_Filename`) is the look; the dials move and scale it. From the corpus:
**Direction** `none` (46%, 5 seq / 4 songs: the picture sits still), `right` (25%, 3 / 2) and
`left` (10%) scroll it across continuously, `up`, `up once` / `down once` pass through once,
`down-left` drifts diagonally, `vector` (5%, one seq) travels from XC / YC to
**PicturesEndXC / EndYC**; **Speed** 1.0 (81%; .7 / .8 slower, 0 frozen in place, 1.6 / 2.0
faster); **FrameRateAdj** is the GIF's playback multiplier (1.0 in 43%, 0.5 half speed on the
Joy Glitter gif, 0.0 freezes the first frame — 27%, 3 seq / 2 songs, used on PNGs where it is
moot; 12.4 once); **Scaling** `Scale To Fit` (51%, 4 / 3, stretched to the matrix), `Scale
Keep Aspect Ratio` (28%, 4 / 3), `No Scaling` (15%, the Danger Zone sleigh pixel for pixel),
`Scale Keep Aspect Ratio Crop` (5%, 2 / 2); **StartScale / EndScale** 100 → 100 in most, 60 →
60 for the sleigh, 100 → 0 for the Light of Christmas tree that shrinks away; **XC / YC** 0 / 0
in 87% / 95% (centred; 19 / 28 for the two angel trumpets, −1 / −21 for the amen); **LoopGIF**
on in 20% (2 seq / 1 song, Light of Christmas), **SuppressGIFBackground** on in 54% (4 / 3);
**TransparentBlack** off in all 137 (the Danger Zone 300 is the threshold with the box off,
moot); **WrapX**, **PixelOffsets** and the picture's own **Shimmer** off everywhere. Not from
the corpus, xLights' behaviour: Transparent Black makes pixels darker than the threshold see
through to the layer below; Wrap X lets a scrolling picture re-enter as it leaves; Pixel Offsets
makes XC / YC pixels instead of percent; the palette is not used — the image carries its own
colours — so the miner's palette and sparkle rows for Pictures are noise (the Sounding Joy
#010101 and the Danger Zone red + black mean nothing on screen).

Role: **feature** on the matrix, the story-telling layer: a snowman, a snowplow, angel wings, a
nativity, a Christmas tree, a sleigh crossing. Also a **texture** (the Joy Glitter gif as a
2-beat sparkle stamp every bar of a chorus) and a **twin** (15 pairs, 0% identical, 20%
differing by transform: the same GIF scrolling left and right at once). 52% share the model
(4 seq / 3 songs); 66 of 137 are alone. Six sequences on five songs but four sequencers and a
handful of files: the anchors are what those people did with their own images.

### Thematic fit

The literal image: when the song names a thing, the matrix shows it. Light of Christmas puts a
snowman on the intro, a snowplow in verse 2, angel wings, praying hands and an "amen" through
bridge 3, a tree on the chorus; Sounding Joy a wing, a nativity and a glitter; Danger Zone a
sleigh across the matrix seven times. Halloween on our hero matrix: a ghost GIF drifting `up
once`, a bat scrolling `right` and flipped on the way back (the sleigh pattern), a pumpkin that
grows (StartScale 10 → EndScale 100, not from the corpus, the reverse of the shrinking tree), a
glitter GIF as the chorus stamp, a tombstone still under an On `2 is Unmask` for a colour
wash. Christmas: the corpus's own set. The file is the thematic dial; the movement is the
phrase. The sequencing skill keeps media effects (Text, Pictures, Video) on a matrix.

**How to permute.** Change the file with the lyric, never mid-phrase. Keep a GIF to one
movement per placement and pair it with its mirror (left + right, up once + down once). Flip
the transform on alternate passes of a scrolling still (the sleigh). Shrink (100 → 0) at the
end of a section, grow at the head. Scale To Fit for a glitter or a full-frame still, Keep
Aspect Ratio for a thing with a shape.

### Prop fit and render style

Matrix 96% (6 seq / 5 songs), one singing-faces mega tree (4%). A picture needs pixels in both
axes; on a line or the whole house it is a smear. The default buffer in every placement;
`Flip Horizontal` is the only transform (11%, 3 / 3). On our layout: the hero seed matrices
(`SEM_FOCAL`), never the video panel, which is reserved for the film. A matrix the size of a
P10 panel shows a GIF; a 16-wide seed matrix shows a glitter or a silhouette, so pick the file
for the pixel count.

### Timing

7 beats 20% (the Light of Christmas bridge: one picture every ~3.3 s), 4 / 3 / 2 beats 15% each
(the sleigh crossings 3–4 beats, the glitter stamp 2–3 beats), 8 beats 13% (the chorus tree),
19 beats once (the nativity still). Median 2.40 s. The glitter stamp lands on every bar of a
Sounding Joy chorus (1:12, 1:14, 1:17 …), lit for two beats of four. Fades .25 / .25 on the
story GIFs, 0 / 1.00 on the stamp, 3 / 0 and 1 / 0 on the stills.

### Anti-patterns

- **A still held for a section with no twin, no scroll and nothing over it.** 66 of 137 are
  alone, but the long ones (the nativity) sit under an On; a bare PNG for 30 s is a sign.
- **A picture off the matrix.** 96% matrix; on a line or `SEM_ALL` it is colour noise.
- **A GIF with FrameRateAdj 0.** Frozen on its first frame; 0.0 is for PNGs.
- **Scale To Fit on a thing with a shape.** The sleigh is `No Scaling`, the tree Keep Aspect
  Ratio; stretch only the glitter.
- **A file on someone else's disk.** Every corpus path is the sequencer's own folder; the file
  must be in our show folder.
- **A palette on a Pictures layer.** It does nothing; the colours are in the image.
- **Our own pipeline: nothing.** It never places a Pictures, so the risk is the opposite: a
  Halloween brief that names a bat or a ghost on the matrix gets nothing until the path below
  exists.

### What code can realize today

- **Nothing places a Pictures.** It is in `ASSET_BOUND_TYPES`
  (`xlights_core/knowledge/constants.py`: Faces, Pictures, Video, Shader, DMX) and has no
  catalog looks, so `stack.expand_stack` drops a layer whose type has no ranked looks
  (`_layer_plan`); the emitter's asset-bound branch knows only Faces
  (`effect_emitter._ASSET_BOUND`) and the direct-settings allowlist only Text and Faces
  (`direct_settings.DIRECT_TYPES`, with Pictures deliberately out).
- **The path that would work** is the one `pipeline/matrix_video.py` takes for the film: a
  per-song JSON naming the target matrix and the image, a settings string built with
  `serialize_settings` (`E_FILEPICKER_Pictures_Filename`, Direction, Scaling, Speed,
  FrameRateAdj, XC / YC, StartScale / EndScale, LoopGIF, SuppressGIFBackground; the facts table
  strips the `E_CHOICE_` / `E_TEXTCTRL_` / `E_SLIDER_` / `E_CHECKBOX_` prefixes, so read the
  full keys off a corpus placement before building it), emitted as one `EffectInstruction`
  with `look_id=""` and `direct_settings`, plus "Pictures" in the emitter's asset-bound set.
  The image must sit in the show folder where the sandboxed xLights can read it, as the mp4
  does. Unlike the film, a picture is a timed placement (2–8 beats at a lyric), so the JSON
  would carry a list of `{file, start_ms, end_ms, direction, scaling}` or hang off a section's
  `key_moments`.
- **Until then**, put the story on the matrix with what places: a Text caption, a Shape, a
  Kaleidoscope over a Pinwheel; and keep the film on the video panel.
- `effect_meta.py` has no Pictures row (no energy band, speed or direction); `blend`,
  `transform` and the fades would carry over as fields, and the buffer Rotation curve as
  `settings`, once a placement path exists.
