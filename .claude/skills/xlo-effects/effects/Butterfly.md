# Butterfly

The organic bed: a slow Palette-coloured interference weave (Style 1, Chunks 1, Skip 2, Speed 10) held 5–8 beats or a whole phrase on rooflines, trees, snowflakes and arches, with the hits — Shockwave, VU Meter, Morph — layered beneath it.

## Craft

### Identity and role

Butterfly renders the "butterfly curve": an interference pattern of colour bands that slowly
shifts. **Colors** `Palette` (90%, 13 seq / 8 songs) paints the bands from the palette;
`Rainbow` (10%, 4 seq / 4 songs) uses the hue wheel. **Style** 1–10 picks the pattern: 1 is the
classic weave (64%, 10 seq / 7 songs), 2 a denser one (16%, 5 seq / 2 songs), 4 the fine ripple
used at Speed 20–30 (15%, 3 seq / 3 songs), 3 (4%, 5 seq / 4 songs); 5–10 never appear except 8
once. **Chunks** repeats the pattern across the buffer (1 in 88%; 2 in 3 seq; 10 in one file for
a fine texture on the mega tree); **Skip** is 2 in every placement; **Speed** is the drift rate
(10 in 52%, 13 seq / 9 songs; 5 for intros; 20–30 with Style 4; 39–40 fast, one sequence each);
**Direction** Normal 98%, Reverse in 2 seq. Blur 7–15 (one sequence) softens the bands on a tree.

Roles: **bed** first and almost only. 129 of 292 placements are alone, median 3.05 s but 22% run
8 s or more, and it is never cut per beat (under a second 3%). Beneath features at Normal: a
Spirals twin, a SingleStrand chase, a Color Wash, a Wave, Meteors. Above the hit layers with
`Layered` in the Christmas Just Ain't Christmas file (Butterfly `Layered` over Shockwave 43, VU
Meter 30, Morph 21, Bars 9): the bed stays on top and the hit shows through it, where a Normal
Butterfly, which fills its buffer, would hide the hit. A **masked bed** in two files: `2 is Mask`
under a Curtain (the curtain wipes the bed away at a section end), `2 is True Unmask` over an On
(the On gates when the bed shows). Never a carrier, never an accent.

### Thematic fit

Butterfly says *organic, aurora, oil on water, a shimmering cloth*: the quiet behind everything.
The palette sets the mood: pinks and violets (Kid On Christmas #FF80FF #80FFFF #8000FF; Christmas
Just Ain't Christmas #8080FF #FF0080 #0000FF #FF00FF), red/green/white gradients for a carol
chorus, Rainbow for a children's song. Halloween: orange, purple and black at Speed 5–10, Style 1
on the roofline and the tombstones; green and purple with Blur 10 on the matrix as fog. Black in
the palette (17%, 6 seq / 4 songs) opens gaps in the weave so a layer beneath shows. Sparkles on
35% (5 seq / 4 songs): the pros put palette sparkles on a Butterfly more than on anything else in
these files.

### Prop fit and render style

Rooflines 18% (6 seq / 6 songs), mega tree 17% (6 seq / 5 songs), snowflakes 15% (4 seq / 4
songs), small trees 13% (2 seq), arches 9% (3 seq / 3 songs), matrix 5%, stars 5%, whole-house
groups 5% (6 seq / 6 songs). On a line (roofline, arch) the weave is a slow colour crawl along
it: that is the roofline bed. On a tree or matrix it is the full pattern, where Blur belongs.
`Per Model Per Preview` on flake and tree groups so each prop gets a whole weave; `Per Preview`
for one weave across the outline. Rob's layout: roofline and tombstones at Per Model Per
Preview, the matrix with Blur; not the characters (a character wants a face, not a weave).

### Timing

5 beats (22%), 6 (13%), 8 (12%), re-cut with the palette (Christmas Just Ain't Christmas: 5–6
beat cells, fades 0.2 / 0.2); 30-plus beats held (10%) with fades 1 / 1; 17 beats for the
Reverse feature; a 2–15 s fade out to leave a section. It abuts; it is never re-cut on the beat.

### Anti-patterns

- **Re-cut on the beat** (ours, when the weave treats it as cellable): a bed that stutters.
  5 beats minimum.
- **Speed 40+ with Chunks 10.** A boiling texture; one of the two.
- **Rainbow on a Christmas or Halloween song.** 10%, and the Palette carries the theme.
- **Butterfly at Normal on top of a hit layer.** The ring is gone. Put the bed at the bottom, or
  use `Layered`.
- **Skip not 2.** Nobody moves it.
- **Butterfly as the only thing on the hero for a chorus.** It is the bed; put the twin or the
  chase on it.
- **Eight colours plus sparkles on a line prop.** Mud.
