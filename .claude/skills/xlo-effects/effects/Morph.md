## Craft

### Identity and role

Morph draws a line between two points and moves it to a line between two other points over
the effect. In the corpus it is used for exactly one thing in 95% of placements: a **wipe**. The
start line runs along the bottom of the buffer (X 0 → 100 at Y 0) and the end line along the
top (Y 100), so a band of color rises through the prop and leaves it lit or dark behind. The
dials: **Duration** (0–90) is the share of the effect's length the head takes to cross (20 =
fast head, long tail; 50 = half-and-half; 65 = slow); **Start Length / End Length** is the
band's thickness at each end (1 = a thin line, 41 = a wide band); **Accel**, **Stagger**,
**Repeat Count** and **Show Head At Start** are left at zero. Reversing the Y values wipes down.

Roles: **carrier** (a two-beat wipe up every cane, re-cut all song), **bed** (a Per Preview
wipe across the whole house with `Layered`, or the floor under a Spirals Brightness stack), and
**transition** (one wipe down the house into a blackout). It is not a feature and not an accent.
Only 10 of 22 sequences use it; it is a sequencer's habit, not a universal, but where it
appears it appears hundreds of times.

### Thematic fit

A wipe says *rising* and *filling*: the house fills from the ground up on a swell, a cane fills on
every beat, a curtain lifts. Down-wipes say *settling* and *falling*. It is quiet and clean, which
is why it carries ballads (Away In A Manger, Christmas Just Ain't Christmas) and R&B (Save Your
Tears) rather than rock. Lyrics about rising, lifting, filling, dawn, tide, or a curtain want it;
anything that needs a shape or a spin does not.

**How to permute.** Change the color per section, nothing else, while it carries. Change the
Duration between sections (20 in the verse, 50 in the chorus so the band lingers). Flip up to
down for the outro. Put two layers at once (Idina Menzel) for a fuller wipe. The pros do not
vary Morph within a section; its job is to be steady under something else.

None on the effect's own dials in the corpus. The only curves are the in/out transition adjust

### Prop fit and render style

Small trees 19%, mega tree 17%, whole-house groups 14%, house outline 13%, canes 13%, arches
4%, floods 4%, windows 3%. `Per Model Per Preview` on groups so every prop wipes alone; `Per
Preview` for the house wipe. Needs height to read: on a one-row roofline it is a flash.

### Timing

Two beats is the rule (58%); one beat on fast songs. Re-cut for whole sections (Save Your
Tears: 22 and 21 in a row). Fades 0.

### Anti-patterns

- **Morph as a feature.** It is a floor. Nothing in the corpus puts it on top of a stack.
- **Varying it inside a section.** It is steady by design; vary the layers above it.
- **A single Morph on a short prop with Duration 65.** On a cane it is a slow blink.
- **Our own Morph share.** 0.7% of our placements versus 6% of the community's: we skip the
  one effect that makes a ballad bed without a texture.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knob_values`: `Morph#23` / `#7` (thin wipe, Duration 0–90), `#10` (band wipe
  with Length 1–41), `#11` (Layered house wipe), `#2` (the transform for a twin). The Y values
  are knobs on every look, so a down-wipe is a knob change.
- `effect_meta.py` classes Morph as a `phrase` effect in energy band 2–4 with no direction or
  speed key; nothing synthesizes it as a carrier or a bed. Write it as a `stack` layer with
  `cell_beats: 2` for the carrier, or `role: "bed"` for the floor.
