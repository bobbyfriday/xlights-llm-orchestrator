## Craft

### Identity and role

Off paints the buffer black and holds it. It has one effect dial, **Style**
(`E_CHOICE_Off_Style`): absent in 83% of placements, `Black -> Transparent` in 17% (1 seq / 1
song, always with Canvas on). Everything else about an Off is a layer property: its length, its
blend, its fades, its transition, its buffer. It never moves, never has a shape, and the colors
it carries (one color 79%, a six-stop gradient in 132 placements) do nothing — they are
leftovers from an On the sequencer converted, or the palette of the stack it sits in.

The corpus is 11 seq / 9 songs, 429 placements, and two files (Magic 206, Uptown Funk 66) are
most of it. It uses Off five ways:

- **Placeholder** (278 placements, 9 seq / 9 songs, no settings at all): a blank layer that
  holds a slot in a stack. 67% of Offs share the model with a partner; beneath an On
  `2 is Unmask` (80 pairs), beneath a Twinkle (26), Meteors (14), Morph (14) or Spirals (9) at
  Normal. The `Off > On > *Off` stack (77) is Magic's stamp sandwich.
- **Beat kill**: a 1–2 beat Off re-cut on a yard group (1 beat 17%, 2 beats 12%) — Uptown
  Funk's mini trees go dark for two beats every bar through the turnaround.
- **Park**: a 16–57 beat Off on one prop while the rest of the display plays (32 beats 16%,
  57 beats 7%, 16 beats 6%; 56% of placements are 8 s or longer). Sounding Joy parks the
  mega-tree star for a verse at a time, Uptown Funk the mini trees for the bridge.
- **Gate**: Off with `Max` or `1 reveals 2` above an On, Shader or Video (On beneath Off: 16
  `1 reveals 2`, 9 Max; Video 6 Max) — 1 seq / 1 song each.
- **Canvas node** (Magic, 2025.08, 1 seq / 1 song): Off on the TOP layer with
  `T_CHECKBOX_Canvas=1`, Style `Black -> Transparent`, `T_LayersSelected`, blend Max /
  `1 reveals 2` / Brightness, 27–32 beats over a ten-layer arch stack. The corpus shows the
  settings only; what Canvas does with them is xLights compositing (not from the corpus) and
  the catalog freezes it as designed looks (`Off#13`, `#8`, `#12`, `#14`, `#15`, `#9`).

Roles: a **mask** tool (placeholder beneath a stamp, `2 is Mask` between a Twinkle and a
Pinwheel, 13 placements, 1 seq / 1 song), an **accent** in reverse (the beat kill), and the
section-long **absence** of a bed on one prop. Never a bed, never a feature, never a carrier
on more than one group at once (whole-display groups are 2%, 3 seq / 3 songs).

### Thematic fit

Off says *not here* or *not yet*. A two-beat kill on the trees is a rest in the rhythm; a
parked star makes the chorus star matter when it lights; a 1 s fade into black on one prop is
the gentlest way to thin the house before a build. Its colors mean nothing, so the Halloween
reading is all placement: kill the stakes for two beats on the drum break, park the cat
characters through a verse so the hero matrix owns it, fade the whole yard except the hero to
black over 1 s before the drop. None of that needs a color on the layer.

**What a brief should use instead of Off for silence: no effect.** A group that should be dark
for a section is simply not in the section's `target_groups` (or the stack's `groups`), and a
quiet section is `treatment: rest`. Code never places an Off on its own (`_castable_rows` drops
an explicit Off from a scene stack; `effect_meta` has no Off row), so an Off layer in a brief
is a deliberate, visible decision: kill *this group's own* bed or stack for a phrase while a
layer on another element keeps running, darken a sub-buffer, or hold a slot beneath a stamp.
Whether an Off on one element overrides a group effect painted on the same pixels is decided
by xLights' render order (the later element wins), not by the Off (not from the corpus).

**How to permute.** There is nothing to permute on the Off; permute *where* and *how long*.
Verse: park one prop (the star, the characters) for 16 beats. Chorus: nothing parked. Drum
break: 2-beat kills on the stakes, `alternation: "chase"` so the dark walks left to right.
Pre-drop: a 1 s fade-in Off on every group but the hero. Between sections change the fade
(cut in the verse, 1 s in the bridge) and the transition (a Wipe for a leaving section).

### Value curves

None: 0% of Off placements carry a curve (0 seq / 0 songs), and Off has no dial a curve could
drive. Its envelope is `fade_in` / `fade_out` and the transition (`T_CHOICE_Out_Transition_Type`
Wipe or Circle Explode, `T_CHECKBOX_Out_Transition_Reverse`, `T_SLIDER_Out_Transition_Adjust
50`). A `TT=Beats` Timing Track curve on any effect only works after `xlo finalize` patches the
Beats track into the sequence; `xlo apply` has no tracks — irrelevant to Off, which never needs
one.

### Prop fit and render style

Canes 26% (6 seq / 6 songs), mega tree 15% (7 / 7), spinners 10% (8 / 7), house props 9%
(4 / 4), stars 8% (5 / 5), mini trees 8% (6 / 6), arches 6% (4 / 4), snowflakes 5% (7 / 7).
The whole display is 2% (3 seq / 3 songs): the pros park a prop, they do not kill the house.
Buffer style does not matter for a flat black except with a Wipe, where `Per Preview` (2 seq /
2 songs) runs the wipe across the real yard rather than per prop. On our layout: a beat kill
on `SEM_STAKE` groups or the characters, a park on `SEM_CHARACTER` or a seed matrix, never on
`SEM_ALL` (leave `SEM_ALL` out of the section instead). The hero matrix never carries an Off;
it carries nothing, or video.

### Timing

Two populations and almost nothing between: 1–2 beats (29%, the kills, re-cut per bar) and
16–57 beats (8–16 s 32%, ≥16 s 24%: the parks and the canvas nodes). Median 9.5 s. Kills abut
at 0. Parks fade in .5–1 s (2 seq / 2 songs) and cut out, or wipe out over .4–.95 s, or close
with a 2.8 s Circle Explode (1 seq each). Gates run 1–5 beats and lift on the cue.

### Anti-patterns

- **Off to say silence.** A section with nothing on a group is a group not named in the
  section. An Off layer costs a row per group, a render, and a layer slot, and says nothing
  the absence did not. Code never synthesizes one; a brief should rarely write one.
- **Off on the whole display.** 2% of the corpus (3 seq / 3 songs). The house goes dark by each
  prop's own ending, or by `treatment: rest`; a black `SEM_ALL` layer under a stack is the
  samey "dark then on" cut our pipeline already makes too often.
- **A 4–8 beat Off.** 3% of the corpus: too long for a rest, too short for a park. Kill for 1–2
  beats or park for 16+.
- **A look from the pick pool.** The pool rotates `Off#0`, `#4` (Blur 4), `#6` (Fadein knob),
  `#1`, `#15` (Canvas on) and `#10` (a reversed Wipe out with fade .95): a bare Off layer with
  no `look_id` can come out as a canvas node or a wiping fade. Name `Off#0`.
- **Expecting an Off at Normal above a layer to black it out.** The corpus puts its hides on
  their own prop (kills, parks) or gives them a blend (Max, `1 reveals 2`, 1 seq / 1 song
  each); the Normal Offs sit *beneath* features as placeholders. Put the Off where the corpus
  puts it, or set the blend.
- **Color on the Off.** 79% carry one color, 132 a gradient; none of it renders. Do not spend a
  palette slot on it.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knobs`: `Off#0` (plain — the one to name), `Off#6` (Fadein as a knob, but
  `fade_in` is a layer field, so write `Off#0` + `fade_in`), `Off#1` (buffer style knob; use the
  `render_style` field), `Off#10` (Wipe-out frozen, knob `T_TEXTCTRL_Fadeout` {.4|.95}),
  `Off#16` (Wipe-in, fade-in 0.40 frozen), `Off#17` (Max + Circle Explode out + 2.80 frozen),
  `Off#2` (Per Model Per Preview + `2 is Mask` frozen), `Off#5` (its knob *is* the LayerMethod
  {2 is Mask|Subtractive} — blend is a field: write `Off#0` + `blend`). `Off#13`, `#8`, `#12`,
  `#14`, `#15`, `#9` freeze `T_CHECKBOX_Canvas=1` and a `T_LayersSelected` list; no stack field
  chooses the selected layers, so they place with Magic's selection, which means nothing on
  our stacks. Never name them.
- `settings`: `E_CHOICE_Off_Style`, `T_CHOICE_Out_Transition_Type` / `_In_`,
  `T_CHECKBOX_Out_Transition_Reverse`, `T_SLIDER_Out_Transition_Adjust`; `blend`, `fade_in`,
  `fade_out`, `transform`, `render_style` are fields; `cell_beats` 1–2 is the beat kill,
  `alternation: "chase"` walks it across the layer's groups.
- `effect_meta.py` has **no Off row**: no speed, no direction, no energy band, no duration
  class, not bed-capable; `render_style.py` falls back to `Default`; `beats.py` lists it in
  `SIMPLE_COLOR`. Nothing synthesizes an Off: `treatment: rest` is a dim bed with no weave and
  no accents, not black; `scenes.py` `_castable_rows` drops an explicit Off from a cookbook
  stack. The gate, the mask hole and the canvas node are not synthesized; the gate is a stack
  layer with `blend`, the other two are one sequencer's habits.
- Render order (which element wins when an Off on one model meets a group effect on the same
  pixels) is patched by `xlo finalize`, not by a stack layer.
