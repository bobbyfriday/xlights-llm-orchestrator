# Twinkle

Random pixels fading up and down: a two-beat white sparkle pop dropped every couple of bars on the whole house or the tree (Count 2–4, Steps 2–18), and at Count 2–6 with slow Steps the quiet bed under a verse; never a per-beat effect.

## Craft

### Identity and role

Twinkle lights random pixels and fades each one in and out. **Count** is the density (2–100:
2–4 a few glints, 30 a field, 50+ a shimmer); **Steps** the length of each pixel's fade cycle
(2 a flicker, 18–33 a glint, 109–366 a slow breathe); **Re-random** redraws which pixels on
every cycle (on in 61% of placements but off in 15 seq / 11 songs: "on" is a few sequencers'
habit); **Strobe** replaces the fade with on/off (3%, 1 seq); **Style** `New Render Method` (93%,
16 seq / 11 songs) fades smoothly and spreads evenly, `Old Render Method` (5 seq) is the older
look. Buffer style matters more than for most effects: `Single Line` (30%, 5 seq / 5 songs)
treats the whole group as one strand so the glints spread by pixel, not by prop.

Roles: **accent** — the pop: 2 beats (24%) or 1 (15%), 40% under a second, a white burst on the
pickup or the section head, dropped every two bars, never every beat; **bed** — 8–30 beats (25%
of placements run 8 s or more) at Count 2–6 and Steps 33–109, one colour, usually the tree, often
under a Spirals; **sparkle layer** over a bed at Normal (Twinkle > Spirals 21, Twinkle > On >
Shader 8); and in one or two sequences a **reveal** (`1 is Unmask` with a 3.5 s fade-in) or an
`Additive` glint. 42% of placements are alone (161) or over an Off (59): sparkles on a dark house.

Against palette sparkles: `C_CHECKBOX_Palette_Sparkles` + `C_SLIDER_SparkleFrequency` add white
glints inside any layer's own render (Butterfly runs them in 35% of its placements). Twinkle is
a layer, with its own colour (white plus blacks to thin it), its own timing (a 2-beat pop while
the bed holds), its own buffer (Single Line across the house) and its own blend (coloured by an
On `2 is Unmask`, cut into stripes by a Spirals `1 is True Unmask`). Sparkles for a shimmer
inside an effect; Twinkle when the sparkle is an event of its own or the only thing lit.

### Thematic fit

Twinkle says *stars, frost, glitter, embers, fireflies*. White on a dark house is the night
sky; red at Count 2, Steps 33 on the tree (Believer) is embers; #8080FF (20 placements) is
frost. It serves intros and verses, the breath before a chorus (the pop), carols (Sounding Joy
drops it between phrases all song), ballads (Save Your Tears, Away In A Manger). Halloween:
orange embers (Count 2–3, Steps 100+) on the tombstones, a green glint coloured by an On
`2 is Unmask`, a white Steps-2 flicker as the answer to a thunder hit. One colour in 61% of
placements and white in 161; black as a colour in 20% (3 seq): three blacks to one white leaves
a quarter of the glints visible, which is how a Count-2 pop gets thinner still.

### Prop fit and render style

Mega tree 35% (8 seq / 7 songs), whole-house groups 22% (9 seq / 8 songs), snowflakes 9%
(5 seq), rooflines 8% (8 seq / 8 songs), one file's house prop 7%, matrix 4%, wreaths, small
trees, pixels, arches, canes. `Single Line` on SEM_ALL for the pop; `Per Model Per Preview` on
tree and flake groups so each prop gets its share; `Default Model As A Pixel` for prop blinks.
Rob's layout: SEM_ALL Single Line for the pop, tombstones and roofline for embers, the matrix
rarely (4%).

### Timing

Pops: 2 beats (24%) or 1 (15%), fade 0.3–0.5 in and 0.5 out, dropped every two bars (Sounding
Joy choruses every ~2.4 s, Uptown Funk verses every ~4.2 s), or 5 beats on a verse pickup.
Beds: 8, 14, 16, 30 beats with the colour re-cut at 4–8 beats; fade in 0.25–1, out 1–2. Never
one-beat re-cuts in a row.

### Anti-patterns

- **Every beat.** The corpus drops it every two bars; per-beat Twinkle is noise.
- **Count 50 at Steps 2 on the whole house.** A strobe with a Twinkle label.
- **Count 30 as the only layer on a chorus canvas.** A screensaver.
- **Four saturated colours.** 61% one colour; more is confetti.
- **White Twinkle over a bright wash.** Invisible; put it over an Off or a dark bed, or colour it
  with an On `2 is Unmask`.
- **Palette sparkles on the bed and a Twinkle layer on top.** Double sparkle.
- **Ours: a Twinkle bed at Count 20+ under everything** because the energy band (1–3) makes it the
  quiet-section pick. The pros' bed is Count 2–6.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knobs`: `Twinkle#3` (Count 2–55, Steps 2–400, Re-random, buffer incl. Single Line)
  is the free look for pops and strands; `#14` (Count 2–10, Steps 30–366) the embers and the
  flake glint; `#4` (Count 2–30, Steps 30–33) the dense glow; `#8` (Count 2–50, Steps 22–33,
  Strobe as a knob); `#1` (Strobe, Style, buffers incl. Default Model As A Pixel); `#27` the head
  flicker with its Wipe; `#28` the reveal (blend frozen); `#24` / `#29` Additive. pick_pool: `#3
  #14 #1 #8 #4 #26`.
- Fields: `render_style: "Single Line"`, `blend: "Additive"` / `"1 is Unmask"`, `fade_in` /
  `fade_out`, `palette: ["white", "black", "black", "black"]`, `brightness`.
- `settings`: the Count and Steps curve strings.
- `effect_meta.py`: an energy band (1–3) and nothing else: no speed, no direction, not cellable.
  The weave takes it as a quiet-section texture and never as a carrier.
- Not synthesized: the every-two-bars pop (a layer holds the section or re-cuts every N beats
  without gaps; there is no "2 beats on, 6 off" — `cell_beats: 8` with `alternation: "sparse"`
  lights a subset of groups per cell but holds the whole cell); the colour-per-phrase bed (one
  palette per layer); the On `2 is Unmask` colouring (write the On as its own layer above with
  `blend`).
