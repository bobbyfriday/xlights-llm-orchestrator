## Craft

### Identity and role

**Count** discs of **Size** drifting around the buffer at **Speed**. The checkboxes change what
a disc is: **Plasma** fills each disc with a plasma colour mix instead of a flat colour (37%,
2 seq / 2 songs); **Linear Fade** fades each disc from its centre to its rim, a soft orb (30%,
one sequencer); **Radial** draws concentric rings from the centre instead of drifting discs (one
placement); **Bubbles** (hollow rings), **Bounce** (off the edges), **Collide**, **Random**
motion and **Radial 3D** are 0 in 100% of the corpus, so what they look like is not from the
corpus: the pros used the plain drifting disc. Count 6 in 37% (2 seq / 2 songs), 10 one
sequencer's, 4–8 the rest (range 1–10). Size 3 (one sequencer, 33%), 15 (one), 20 (2 seq /
2 songs), 5 (2 seq / 2 songs), 1. Speed 10 in 44% (3 seq / 3 songs); curved in 33%, all one
sequencer's Ramp 5 → 20. One colour in 59%, two in 37%: purple (6), orange (5), purple + green,
red + green, red + yellow, white.

Roles: a 2–4 beat **gesture** on the matrix (33%, one sequencer: Tammy Ghee) or the whole house
(37%, 3 seq / 3 songs) at a section head, usually over an On; a 14–16 beat **feature** on the
house once (Baby Shark, orange); a colour drift on the floods (11%, one sequencer); the thing
beneath a Pinwheel or Shockwave (3 + 2). 48% share the model (4 seq / 4 songs) and no placement
is twinned with itself. Never a bed in the corpus beyond Baby Shark's 30 beats, never a mask or
a carrier; `Circles` is not cellable and not bed-capable in `effect_meta`.

### Thematic fit

Discs say *balls*, *orbs*, *bubbles*, *balloons*, *fireflies*, *eyes*. The corpus's own colours
are already half Halloween: purple (Kid On Christmas, Jeremy Poling), purple + green (Tammy
Ghee's matrix), orange Size 20 (Baby Shark). Orange discs at Size 20 and Count 8 drifting across
the yard are pumpkins; purple + green Plasma dots are spook-lights; a Linear Fade purple orb per
stake is a will-o'-the-wisp over every grave; a white Count 1 Radial ring is a sonar ping or a
portal opening. Not from the corpus: Bubbles on with Size 15 is literal soap bubbles for a bath
or baby song; Bounce on with Count 2–4 is bouncing balls for a lyric that says so. Christmas:
red + green discs on a tree are baubles; the corpus used them as a two-beat garnish.

**How to permute.** Change the colour pair every occurrence (Tammy did: purple + green, red +
green, red + yellow across three section heads) and keep Count 6 Size 3. Size per section: 3 on
a verse head, 15–20 for the chorus. `Per Model Per Preview` on the house so every prop carries its
own orbs; the default buffer so a few big discs cross the yard. Add the speed ramp for a head that
should accelerate, drop it for a drift. Turn on Bounce or Bubbles only for a lyric that names
them (not from the corpus).

### Prop fit and render style

Whole-house groups 37% (3 seq / 3 songs), the matrix 33% (one sequencer), floods 11% (one),
tree toppers and mini-tree stars 11% (2 seq / 2 songs), a snowflake group and a pixel field
once. A disc needs area: on the matrix it is a disc; on the house with the default buffer
(Baby Shark) a few big discs cross the yard and each prop lights as one passes; with `Per Model
Per Preview` (3 seq / 3 songs) every prop has its own set, which is how the orbs read on small
props. On a flood or a topper a disc is the prop blinking a colour. On the Halloween layout: the
Plasma dots on the hero matrix, `Per Model Per Preview` orbs across the stakes so each tombstone
has its wisp; a disc on a cat is a blink, so leave the characters out.

### Anti-patterns

- **Circles as a section bed.** Baby Shark's 30 beats is the longest hold; the corpus is a
  2-beat gesture. Our pipeline treats it as a free-duration energy 2–3 feature and will hold
  it the whole section: give it `cell_beats` or keep it to a short section.
- **Static Speed 25 on a chorus.** The pipeline's speed key runs 5–25 by intensity; the corpus's
  fast Circles is the Ramp 5 → 20, not a flat 25 (one placement).
- **Count 10, Size 20, default buffer on the house.** One blob.
- **Three colours.** One placement; one or two.
- **Bounce, Collide and Bubbles on by default.** Nobody; every one is 0 in 100%.
- **Confusing the Plasma checkbox with the Plasma effect.** This one fills a disc; the effect
  is a bed.
