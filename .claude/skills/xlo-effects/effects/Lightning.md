## Craft

### Identity and role

A jagged bolt drawn across the buffer and flickered for the life of the effect, re-cut every half
second or so while the storm lasts. From the corpus: **Number_Bolts** 10–12 and
**Number_Segments** 5–11 (10 / 5 on the Danger Zone mega tree, 11 / 7 on its whole-house
overlay, 12 / 6 and 10 / 11 in Jeremy Poling's two uses); **TopX** is where the bolt starts,
offset from centre (0 in 42%, 2 seq / 2 songs; 19 / −3 / −23 for the three simultaneous
Danger Zone bolts; ±50 in Jeremy's pair), **BOTX** where it lands (0 in 88%; ±50 in Jeremy's
pair, the opposite sign to its TopX, so each bolt leans across the buffer); **TopY** 0 and
**BOTY** 0 everywhere; **ForkedLightning** on in Danger Zone's 27 (82%) and off in Jeremy's 6;
**WIDTH** absent in Danger Zone, 3 or 1 in Jeremy's; **Direction** `Up` in all 33. Not from
the corpus, xLights' behaviour: Top X/Y and Bottom X are percent offsets of the two ends,
Segments is how jagged the path is, Bolts how many bolts flash during the effect, Forked adds
side branches, Width the line thickness, and the bolt redraws at random so no two frames match.

Role: **accent**, and only that: median 0.57 s, 91% under a second; the one 4-beat placement is
a green bolt over an On and a Galaxy. 61% share the model, and the partner is mostly another
Lightning (35 of 46 pairs): the idiom is **three bolts at once** on three layers (Danger Zone,
15 of the 33), differing by TopX and colour, with a 71% rate of opposite-sign TopX between the
two layers of a twin. Two sequences on two songs: the anchors below are two sequencers' habits,
and the rest is labelled xLights behaviour.

### Thematic fit

A storm, a power chord, a rock stinger, a spell landing, the moment the monster appears. The
sequencing skill names it for "storm / rock stingers" and caps hit effects at a bar. Halloween:
white + purple bolts on the whole yard at the scream, a single purple bolt down the hero matrix
on a drum fill, an orange bolt over the tombstone stakes at the chorus head. Red + blue + white
is Danger Zone's set (9 red, 9 blue, 5 white + red); green for a Shut Up and Dance stinger; pink
+ purple / purple / cyan / teal for Jeremy's four-colour staggered pair. One colour per layer
(82%); the second colour is the fork.

**How to permute.** Three layers, three TopX values, three colours (Danger Zone). Re-cut the
stack every 0.4–0.8 s while the stinger lasts and shorten the fade out with the cut. On the
next stinger, change the lean (opposite-sign TopX / BOTX) rather than the bolt count. Use
`Rotate CC 90` on the house (a horizontal bolt across the roofline) and none on the tree.

### Value curves

None observed (0 of 33). The bolt moves by itself; it needs no curve.

### Prop fit and render style

Whole-house groups 61% (2 seq / 2 songs), mega tree 36%, one flood. `Overlay - Centered` with
`Rotate CC 90` lays one bolt across the whole display; `Per Model Per Preview` draws a bolt on
every prop at once; the default buffer on the tree draws it top to bottom. It needs height: on a
line prop the bolt is a dash. On the Halloween layout: the hero matrix (default buffer), the
tombstone stakes `Per Model Per Preview` (a bolt on every stone), `SEM_ALL` with the
sideways overlay for the scream. Not on the video panel.

### Timing

Median 0.57 s; 91% under a second. Danger Zone re-cuts the triple bolt five times in 3 s
(0.7, 0.6, 0.6, 0.4, 0.8 s). Jeremy's are 1 beat each, staggered by 0.2 s. Fade out .1–.5. The
house rule from `xlo-sequencing`: hit effects are ≤ 1 bar.

### Anti-patterns

- **Lightning as a bed.** One 4-beat placement in 33; past a bar it is a flicker.
- **Lightning every beat.** The corpus fires it at a stinger and re-cuts within the stinger.
- **One layer, one colour.** The Danger Zone look is three layers; a lone bolt on a group canvas
  is a scratch.
- **The same TopX on every layer.** Three bolts in one place read as one.
- **`Lightning#0` from the picker on the tree.** It bakes in `Rotate CC 90` and
  `Overlay - Centered`, the sideways house bolt; on a tree that is a horizontal dash. Name `#3`.
- **Our own pipeline: a `hit`-class accent on the beat ring.** `effect_meta` classes Lightning as
  a hit, so a brief that names it as the accent would strike on every accent beat; keep it to a
  key moment.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knobs`: `Lightning#0` (`E_SLIDER_Lightning_TopX` −23…19, `T_TEXTCTRL_Fadeout`
  .1 / .3 / .5; the sideways overlay frozen), `#3` (fade out .4 / .5; the tree bolt), `#1`
  (`E_SLIDER_Lightning_BOTX` and `_TopX` −50…50; Width 3, `Layered` frozen), `#2` (frozen). The
  picker rotates `#0`, `#3`, `#2`. A Width or bolt count outside a look goes in `settings` as
  `E_SLIDER_Lightning_WIDTH` / `E_SLIDER_Number_Bolts` / `E_SLIDER_Number_Segments`.
- `effect_meta`: energy band 3–5, `duration_class="hit"` (so it is in `DURATION_HIT`); no
  speed, no direction. Nothing synthesizes the triple bolt: write three `stack` layers with
  `role: "accent"`, each `look_id: "Lightning#0"` with its own TopX knob and `palette`, over
  the section's bed, or a `twin: "color"` on one layer for two of the three.
