## Craft

### Identity and role

Spirals wraps one or more stripes around the model's buffer as a helix and moves them. On a
tree it reads as a barber pole, a tornado, a climbing vine or a carousel depending on four dials:

- **Count** — how many stripes share the circumference. 1 is a single ribbon; 4–5 are bands.
- **Thickness** — stripe width as a share of its slot. 10 is a thread, 50 is stripe = gap, 98–100
  is a solid cylinder of color (only useful with `Blend` on or a Brightness blend over a bed).
- **Rotation** — the *twist*: how far the ribbon leans across the buffer height. It is NOT spin.
  Small (5–20) is a gentle lean, 50–80 is a tight corkscrew, ±300 wraps many times. The sign is
  the lean direction. This is the dial the pros animate most.
- **Movement** — the *spin*: speed and direction the stripes travel. Sign = clockwise or counter.
  0 is static, 0.5–1 is a slow turn, 2–4 is driving, 10 is a blur.
- **3D** shades each stripe so the cylinder reads as round; **Blend** turns hard bands into a
  gradient. **Grow/Shrink** exist but the corpus never uses them; neither should you.

Roles it plays: **feature** (a twin on the hero is a whole section's identity), **carrier** (1–2
beat cells, colors and twist sign alternating), **bed** (one thick slow layer with Sine or Saw
Tooth twist under everything). It is never an overlay or a mask, and it is not an accent: a hit
goes on top of it (Shockwave, Meteors), it does not become one.

### Thematic fit

Spirals says *turning* and *rising*. Red and white at Thickness 50 on canes and poles is the
candy cane itself, and the single most literal thing the effect does (Light of Christmas ticks it
on every cane every two bars). Green and white or red and green on the tree is the Christmas
default; a twin in pink, cyan and violet is the modern "Kid On Christmas" look; purple and green
with a slow Movement of 0.3–0.5 and a high twist is an ominous Halloween creep; green, white and
red on a black background with fast spin and a Shape mask is the Danger Zone jet-engine look.
Lyrics about climbing, winding, spinning, a carousel, a tornado, "round and round" or a storm
all want it. A song about stillness or falling does not; use Ripple or Meteors there.

**How to permute.** The pros keep an anchor for a whole section and change *one* thing per
phrase: the twist sign (Light of Christmas verse 1 flips which layer leans left every 8 beats),
the color pair (red+green → white+green → red+green), the transform on the twin (180 on verse,
CC 90 / CW 90 on the chorus), or the curve shape (Sine on the bed under verse 2, Timing Track
pump on the chorus). Between sections they change *two*: thickness up and Blend on for the
chorus; thickness down and Blend off for the verse. Never change all four dials at once; the
result reads as a different effect, not a development of this one.

| **Crawl under chase** | SingleStrand (Layered or `2 is Unmask`) · Spirals · Spirals · Morph | as named | roofline / outline |

Blend semantics that make these work: under `Normal`, black pixels of an upper layer are
transparent, which is why a two-color palette with black is so common. `Brightness` treats the
Spirals as a brightness envelope on the layer below, so the bed's colors survive and the stripes
are shape only. `Layered` lets a textured overlay (Shader, Color Wash, SingleStrand) tint what is
below rather than cover it. `2 reveals 1` on a twin lets each ribbon reveal the other where they
cross.

### Value curves

| Want | Curve | Dial |
|---|---|---|
| a build across the pre-chorus | Ramp −300 → 300 (full sweep) or Movement Ramp 20 → 60; add `Circle Explode` in | Rotation / Movement |
| a bed that never sits still | Sine (P2 −200, P3 10) or Saw Tooth ±50 ×1 | Rotation |
| a driving verse | Saw Tooth ±200 ×2 | Rotation |
| the twist snapping on every beat | Timing Track Fade Proportional ±50 on the beat track (±300 for the last chorus) | Rotation |
| a breath per phrase | Ramp Up/Down 0 → 100 → 0 | Thickness |
| a rock back and forth | Ramp Up/Down −100 → 100 → −100 | Rotation |
| a settle after a hit | Ramp 0 → 102 | Rotation |
| the whole buffer turning | B_VALUECURVE_Rotation Ramp 0 → 100 | buffer rotation |

Timing-track curves need a track with the name in `TT=`; our finalize step writes `Beats`, so
write `TT=Beats` for our shows (the community files use `Beat Count`). Verified live 2026-11-02:
after `xlo finalize` patches the track in and the sequence is re-rendered, a `TT=Beats` twist on
a Movement-0 Spirals moves at every beat edge and sits still between them. The render done by
`xlo apply` itself has no Beats track yet, so judge the pump after finalize. When in doubt use
Saw Tooth with `P3` = the number of beats in the cell.

### Prop fit and render style

- **Mega tree / matrix**: the default buffer. `Rotate CW 90` or `CC 90` makes the stripes run
  around the tree instead of up it; `Rotate 180` is the cheap mirror twin. Per Preview only when
  the Spirals should cross a whole-house group as one ribbon (Shut Up and Dance).
- **Small trees, canes, spinners**: `Per Model Default` so every prop spins its own ribbon; the
  corpus does this on 36 of 81 small-tree placements and 59 of 184 cane placements.
- **Rooflines / outlines / arches**: only the Crawl (Count 3, twist 68, Movement curved) or the
  twin at Thickness 100 on arches. Anything thinner vanishes on a line.
- **Stakes / pixel fields**: Per Preview, Count 4–5, long holds.
- Render cost is low; Spirals is safe on `SEM_ALL`, unlike Fan or Shockwave.

### Timing

Cells 1–2 beats (ticks, cascades, fat bars) with fades 0; phrases of 8 beats (twins, builds)
with 0.25 s fades or a Wipe; holds of 16–32 beats (bridge beds, spinners) with 1 s fade in and 0
out. Twins start and end together. A cascade staggers its layers by one beat. The cane tick lands
on the downbeat every two bars.

### Anti-patterns

- **One Spirals layer on a group canvas** at Thickness 50, twist 20, Movement 1: reads as fat
  vertical bars (our own default; Rob: "spirals look like bars"). The pros never place one alone.
- **Same spin, same lean, all song.** The corpus has 17% negative Movement (in 11 of 19
  sequences) and 38% opposite-lean twins; ours had zero. Flip something every phrase.
- **Thickness 98–100 with Normal blend and no bed**: a solid wash that happens to rotate.
  Fat needs `Brightness` over a bed or `Blend` on.
- **Twist curves that only ramp positive from zero.** The pros sweep −300 → 300, rock, saw and
  pump; a one-way ramp from rest is the least used shape.
- **Three or more non-black colors with Blend on** turns to mud on a tree. Two colors plus black
  is the idiom.
- **Grow / Shrink.** Never observed in 1,678 placements.
- **Spirals as the accent.** It is the bed or the feature; the hit goes on top.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knob_values`: every anchor above names a catalog look whose key signature matches;
  knob values are validated against that look's *observed* range (`validators.py`), so a twist
  of 120 on `Spirals#57` (range −66…66) is rejected and the row is skipped. Pick the look whose
  range contains the value, or use the curve instead.
- `extra_settings` is free-form and unvalidated: value curves (`E_VALUECURVE_*`,
  `B_VALUECURVE_Rotation`), `B_CHOICE_BufferTransform`, `T_CHOICE_LayerMethod`,
  `T_TEXTCTRL_Fadein/Fadeout`, `T_CHOICE_In/Out_Transition_Type`, `B_SLIDER_Blur`,
  `C_SLIDER_SparkleFrequency` all go here with the exact strings above. The emitter always sets
  `B_CHOICE_BufferStyle` from `render_style`.
- Code already applies spin sign and twist walks per phrase (`pipeline/motion.py`) and counter-
  rotates composite stacks (`weave.counter_rotate_stacks`); the mirror-twin-by-transform, the
  Brightness-over-Morph fat bars, the beat pump and the breathing thickness are not synthesized
  anywhere; write them as `stack` layers (`twin: "mirror"`, `blend: "Brightness"` over a
  Morph layer, the beat-pump curve in `settings`, the Thickness curve in `settings`).
- Sections without a stack get their Spirals look from the picker (rotating among the most-used
  neutral looks; `#99` is excluded as a designed look). The anchors' looks are `#57`, `#99`, `#88`,
  `#70`, `#80`, `#60`, `#101`; name them in a stack layer.
