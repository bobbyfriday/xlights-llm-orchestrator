## Craft

### Identity and role

A ring that grows (or shrinks) from a point. Five dials shape it: **CenterX / CenterY** place the
origin (50/50 the middle; 0 or 100 an edge or corner); **Start Radius → End Radius** is the
journey; **Start Width → End Width** is how fat the ring is at each end; **Accel** bends the
speed (negative = fast then slow). **Blend Edges** softens the ring and the pros leave it on; the
newer **Scale** box scales the radius to the buffer so one setting fits every prop; **Cycles** is 1.

Two archetypes cover almost every placement:

- **Ring**: Start Radius ~1, End Radius 160–330, Start Width 50–170 → End Width 0–95. A visible
  pulse travels outward and thins away. On `Per Preview` it is one ring crossing the whole yard
  from one point; on the default buffer it is a ring on each prop.
- **Pop** (End Radius 0, End Width 0, Start Width 80–120, Start Radius 0–22): a fat soft disc
  that collapses to nothing in a beat. It reads as a flash with a shape. On `Per Model Default`
  every prop pops on its own. This is the beat marker the community uses instead of On/Strobe.

Roles: **accent** (the hit) is the obvious one, but the bigger role is **carrier**: a pop or ring
on every beat for a whole verse. It also serves as a **mask source** (an On layer above it with
`2 is Unmask` turns the soft ring into a hard flat-color ring) and stacks with itself. It is never
a bed; anything longer than two beats needs the breathing curve or it smears.

### Thematic fit

Shockwave says *impact* and *radiance*: a drum hit, a heartbeat, a bell, a pulse of light from a
star or from the house. White is its native color; red on the downbeat and white elsewhere is
the funk idiom (Uptown Funk); one ring per beat in white over black is the arena-rock idiom
(Believer). It suits any song with a backbeat worth marking, a kick drum, hand claps, bells,
fireworks, a heartbeat lyric, "light", "shine", "burst". It does not suit legato strings or a
lullaby, where a ring on every beat is a metronome nobody asked for.

**How to permute.** Keep the archetype for a section and change the color on the phrase (Uptown
Funk: white, then four beats red, then white). Change the origin between sections (middle in the
verse, from the edge in the chorus, corner stingers in the bridge). Change the buffer between
roles: the pop is per model, the chorus ring is per preview. Never change the width and the
radius and the origin at once; it reads as a different effect.

### Prop fit and render style

Everywhere: small trees 22%, stars 22%, snowflakes 13%, mega tree 11%, house outline 10%,
whole-house group 5%, matrix 5%, canes 3%. Per Model Default for pops so each prop pops alone;
Per Preview for one ring across a group; the default buffer for a ring on the hero. Shockwave is
render-expensive on large grids: keep the yard ring to one placement per beat on the group, not
a copy on every prop.

### Timing

One beat is the rule (half the corpus). Two beats for a ring crossing the yard. Four to eight
beats only with the breathing curve. Pops are re-cut on every beat for a whole section, 28–32 in
a row in Uptown Funk; Believer rings eight beats on, eight off. Fades 0; transitions off.

### Anti-patterns

- **A flat On or Strobe where a ring belongs.** Our shows punctuated with On, Strobe, Shimmer and
  Twinkle 42% of the time; the community does that 7% of the time and rings 13%. Shaped hits read
  as music; flat flashes read as a fault.
- **Shockwave only as a rare "hit".** The pros pop every beat. Treat it as a carrier with a hit's
  shape.
- **A long Shockwave without the breathing curve.** Past two beats a static ring is a smear.
- **A copy on every prop AND the group.** Pick per model or per preview, not both; it doubles the
  render cost and the brightness.
- **A whole-house ring at one beat with End Radius 300.** It crosses the yard in a third of a
  beat and flickers (seen live 2026-11-02). A big moment wants 2 beats to a bar at End Radius
  110–130, so the ring propagates across the entire house.
- **Four colors.** One or two; black as the second.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knob_values`: `Shockwave#10` (prop ring), `#28` (pop: End Radius and End Width
  reach 0), `#26` (floor swell, Brightness blend frozen in), `#18` (per-model ring with fade), `#33`
  (corner burst). The yard ring needs End Radius 285–330, which only `#10`'s range (to 504)
  allows; `Scale` and `Cycles` are keys from the 2024+ files and are not in the catalog yet.
- `extra_settings`: the two Ramp Up/Down curves, `T_CHOICE_LayerMethod=Additive`, `B_SLIDER_Blur`.
  `value_curves.MOTION_CURVES` already knows `("Shockwave", "radius")` and `("Shockwave",
  "width")` as sweep curves.
- `effect_meta.py` classes Shockwave as a `hit` (≤ 1 bar) in energy band 3–5 with no direction
  knob. Code now synthesizes the **big-moment ring** on its own (`pipeline/hits.py`: the strongest
  drum onsets of a section at intensity ≥ 0.6, one per two bars at most, 2 beats, whole display,
  `on_top`, Max). The per-beat pop carrier, the double ring and the hard ring are still authored:
  set `accent_effect: "Shockwave"` in a rhythmic section, or a `stack` layer with
  `role: "carrier"`, `cell_beats: 1` and the pop knobs (`Shockwave#28`, End Radius/Width 0).
