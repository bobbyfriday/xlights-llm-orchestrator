## Craft

### Identity and role

Streaks with tails crossing the buffer. **Effect** is the direction: `Down` (35%, 11 seq /
7 songs: rain, snow), `Up` (29%, 6 seq / 6 songs: sparks, rising), `Explode` (28%, 9 seq /
7 songs: out from a point), `Implode` (4%, 4 seq / 4 songs), `Right` (3%, 4 seq / 4 songs),
`Icicles` (one placement). **Count** is the density (10 in 35%, 11 seq / 7 songs; 1 for a single
comet, 3 seq / 3 songs; 20–28 thick; 77–79 a wall); **Length** the tail (25 in half the corpus,
13 seq / 8 songs; 1–9 short sparks; 52–70 long streaks); **Speed** (10 in 46%; 3–5 a drift;
43–50 a burst); **Swirl Intensity** a side-to-side wobble (0 in 73%; 4 a flicker; 20 a storm);
**Warmup Frames** (`WamupFrames`, xLights' own spelling) pre-runs the field so it is full at
frame one (0 or absent in 87%; 30 for a burst that has to be there on the downbeat); **X / Y
Offset** move the origin of Explode and Implode (0, or ±100 for a corner); **Type** is Palette in
90% (Rainbow in 2 seq / 2 songs, Range once); **Use Music** (18%, 2 seq / 2 songs) lets the
audio level drive the count; **Fade With Distance** dims the tails as they travel (one placement).

The palette is a density dial: a meteor drawn in black is invisible, so `#00FF00+#000000+#000000`
is green rain at a third of the Count. Black is a colour in 34% of placements (4 seq / 4 songs).

Roles: **feature** on the matrix and the whole house (rain or sparks for a phrase); **accent** (a
2–3 beat Explode from a corner, a one-hit burst at Count 79); **carrier** over a Spirals or
Pinwheel bed (42 + 29 pairs beneath it). It is a mask only once (`1 is Unmask`) and a bed almost
never. 68% of placements share the model. Fifteen of 22 sequences and 10 songs use it, the widest
breadth of any effect in this group of files, though Light of Christmas (main) places 43 of 115.

### Thematic fit

Falling says *rain*, *snow*, *tears*, *stars falling*; rising says *sparks*, *embers*,
*fireflies*; exploding says *fireworks*, *a hit*; imploding says *gather*, a breath in before the
drop. Christmas: white or blue rain on the matrix in a verse, red-green-blue snow on the flakes
for a whole verse, green sparks rising on the "owl" chorus (all Light of Christmas). Halloween:
orange + red + black `Up` at Count 6–10 is embers rising from the hero; white `Down` at Speed 3–5
with Length 52 is drizzle in a graveyard; green `Down` with two blacks is ghost rain; `Explode`
from the bottom corners at Swirl 20 is a hit. Rainbow is a party colour (Shut Up and Dance), not a
holiday one. Pair the direction with the lyric: "fall" gets Down, "rise" and "fire" get Up.

to the top corners on consecutive cells. Add Swirl 4 on the second occurrence. Give the twin the
opposite X Offset (half of corpus twins), never identical settings. A Speed ramp 23 → 50 across
the last chorus is the one curve with breadth.

### Prop fit and render style

Whole-house groups 28% (9 seq / 7 songs), mega tree 27% (8 seq / 6 songs), matrix 26% (3 seq /
3 songs), pixel fields 5% (3 seq / 3 songs); everything else is one sequence. Down and Up need
height, Explode needs a 2D buffer. The default buffer on a tree or matrix. `Per Model Default`
(5 seq / 4 songs) on a flake, cane or mini-tree group so each prop gets its own streaks;
`Per Preview` (2 seq / 2 songs) so one field of rain falls across the whole yard with each prop
catching its share, which is the better whole-house rain; `Default Model As A Pixel` once, a rain
where each model is a drop. On the Halloween layout: embers `Up` on the hero and seed matrices,
`Per Preview` rain across the yard including the stakes (a stake is a column and rain runs down
it), `Explode` on the matrix for a hit.

### Anti-patterns

- **Count 10, Speed 10, Length 25 every time.** The community default is a default; the next
  section needs another direction or another density.
- **Three bright colours at Count 28.** A wall of confetti. Thin it with black.
- **Explode held for a phrase.** It is a 2–3 beat head gesture; held, it is static noise. Rain and
  snow are what hold.
- **Identical twin.** A quarter of corpus twins; the rest differ by offset sign. Give the twin the
  opposite X Offset.
- **Down on a roofline with the default buffer.** Nothing falls along a line; use `Per Preview`
  so the roofline catches the rain crossing it.
- **Our own pipeline: Meteors as a 1-beat cell.** It is `cellable` in `effect_meta`, but a 1-beat
  Meteors at Warmup 0 never builds a field: it flickers. Hold it 3–8 beats or set Warmup 30.
- **Use Music on a dense field.** It is for Count 10 over a bed, one colour and two blacks.

  Warmup 0–40); `#21` / `#37` / `#32` (snow); `#31` (music sparks with the Length curve frozen);
  `#18` (the corner explosion, `E_TEXTCTRL_Meteors_XOffset` / `YOffset` ±100); `#17` / `#15`
  (bursts, Layered); `#16` (the Implode gather); `#24` (Rainbow); `#7` / `#35` (the comet with
  `1 is Unmask`); `#6` (Per Model Default, Type Palette / Range); `#34` / `#36` (embers). A Count
  above 10 on a Down field needs `#21` (10–20); above 26 only `#17` / `#15` (Explode).
- `settings`: the curve strings above, `B_SLIDER_Blur`,
  `T_CHOICE_Out_Transition_Type=Circle Explode`; `blend` for `Layered` / `1 is Unmask`;
  `render_style` for Per Preview / Per Model Default; `fade_in` / `fade_out`.
- `effect_meta` gives Meteors a speed key (`E_SLIDER_Meteors_Speed` 10–45), directions `up` /

  `direction: "up"` on a layer sets the Effect; `twin: "counter"` reverses it (Up over Down,
  Explode over Implode), which the corpus never does. The corpus twin (opposite X Offset) is two
  layers on `#18` with `XOffset` −100 and 100, since the counter twin only negates `_Rotation` /
  `_Twist` sliders.
- Nothing synthesizes the black-padded palette, Use Music, or the 2-beat corner explosion at a
  section head; write them.
