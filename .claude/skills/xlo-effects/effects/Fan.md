## Craft

### Identity and role

Blades radiating from a hub and turning. **Num Blades** (3 in 75%, 6 seq / 4 songs; 2 on the
flakes, 4 in the corner burst, 6 in one sequence) and **Blade Width** (100 = the blades touch and
the disc is solid colour bands; 50 and 36 leave black gaps so the bed shows through) make the
wheel; **Blade Angle** (90 in 83%) is the sweep of each blade, 0 makes straight spokes;
**Revolutions** is how far the wheel turns over the effect (360 = one turn, the mode; 180 a half
turn; 720 two; 3600 a ten-turn blur, one sequence); **Start Radius → End Radius** is the ring the
blades grow across while they turn (0 → 21 grows a small wheel out of the hub, 111 → 0 collapses
one into it, 1 → 243 fills the buffer); **CenterX / CenterY** place the hub (50 / 50, or 0 / 0 for
a corner); **Duration** (Options tab) is the share of the effect's time the motion takes, the rest
holds the last frame (100 in 76%; 80 in 5 seq / 4 songs; 74–76 in one); **Accel** is 0 in the
whole corpus; **Reverse** turns it the other way (18%, 3 seq / 2 songs); **Blend Edges** softens
the blade edges (on in 99%). Num Elements splits each blade into sub-blades (1 in 89%; a
1 → 4 → 1 curve in one sequence). `E_NOTEBOOK_Fan` is the panel tab that was open when the
effect was saved, not a setting.

Roles: **feature** (the per-flake propeller, the wheel on a band of the tree) and **accent** (a
2–3 beat burst at a section head). It is a bed only under a Spirals twin in one sequence (24
stacks) and a mask once (`Brightness`, 1%). 70% of placements share the model, and the stack it
lives in is a Fan twin (36 + 36) or three Fans under three Waves (54, one sequence). Nine of 22
sequences use it on five songs; Kid On Christmas (Eliot Wisser) places 169 of the 275, so the
numbers below are his habits unless a wider breadth is given.

### Thematic fit

A fan says *spin*, *burst*, *bloom*, *propeller*, *sun*. Growing from radius 0 it is a flower
opening or a sunburst; collapsing to 0 it is a drain; a half turn in two beats is a wheel
winking; ten turns is a turbine (the Danger Zone files). On a snowflake it is the flake spinning;
on a star it is the star turning; from the corner of a whole-house buffer it is dawn breaking
over the display. Christmas: 2-blade yellow / red / green propellers on the flakes for a verse
(Light of Christmas). Halloween: a 3-blade orange burst from the centre of the hero matrix on a
hit; a purple-and-black wide-gap wheel (Blade Width 36–50) turning slowly on a wreath or
spinner; a corner burst in orange at a section head. The blades take the palette in order, so
three colours give a three-colour disc (64% of placements carry three); a black in the palette
(4%) leaves one blade dark and the bed shows through it.

**How to permute.** Change the blade count between sections (2 in the verse, 3 in the chorus, 6
on the last chorus). Grow in one section and collapse in the next (swap Start and End Radius).
Step Revolutions up as the cells lengthen (180 on the 2-beat cells, 540 on the 4-beat cell, the
Kid On Christmas pattern) rather than changing a speed. Give the twin `Flip Horizontal` or
`Reverse`, never the same settings (57% of corpus twins are identical, which only reads as one
brighter fan). Move the hub: centre in the verse, a corner for the section head, top-and-bottom
in the bridge. Rotate which band of the tree holds the wheel. Keep three colours and let the
palette rotate; the disc is the colour message.

### Prop fit and render style

Mega tree 37% (3 seq / 3 songs), snowflakes 25% (2 seq / 2 songs), stars 9%, matrix 8%, mini
trees 5%, spinners 4%, rooflines 4%, whole-house groups 4% (each 3 seq / 3 songs). Any round or
2D prop; a line prop or a stake gets one moving dot (one placement on Pixel Stakes). On a flake
or star group use `Per Model Default` so each prop gets its own wheel (Light of Christmas), or
`Per Preview` so one big fan across the preview is sliced by the props (Kid On Christmas); on a
tree use the default buffer with a SubBuffer third per layer for the stacked wheels, or the whole
buffer for one big wheel; on a whole-house group a corner hub with Revolutions 0 is a sunburst
that reaches every prop. On the Halloween layout the hero matrix takes the burst, the seed
matrices and any wreath or spinner take the wheel; keep it off the tombstone stakes.

### Timing

2 beats 41%, 4 beats 19%, 3 beats 17%: a bar or less, re-cut, median 1.57 s. The corner burst is
3 beats at a section head and nothing else; the flake propeller is 4-beat cells for a whole
verse; the tree wheels are 2 + 2 + 4 beats per pair of bars, Revolutions stepping up with the
cell. Fades 0 on the cells; 0.25 / 0.25 on the collapsing fan; fade in 1.5 with `Fold` on one
layer per cell, rotating among the three bands (one sequence); 1 / 1 with `Wipe` on the slow
pair. 8–9 beats only for the turbine and the pair.

### Anti-patterns

- **Identical twin.** 57% of corpus twins are the same settings on two layers: a brighter fan,
  nothing else. Give the twin Flip Horizontal, Reverse, or another hub.
- **One fan held for a phrase.** The corpus re-cuts at 2–4 beats; a 16-beat fan at Revolutions
  360 is a slow clock.
- **A fan on a line or a stake.** A dot. Keep it to round and 2D props.
- **Revolutions 3600 on everything.** The turbine is one sequencer's 7 placements; on a small
  prop it strobes.
- **Accel.** Nobody uses it; a Revolutions curve does the wind-up legibly.
- **Our own picker: `Fan#3` first.** Without a stack the pool rotates `#3`, `#2`, `#25`, `#11`,
  `#22`, `#21`, all but one a 3-blade 360 wheel. Name the blade count, the radii and the hub per
  section.
- **The burst on every beat.** The corner sunburst lands once at a section head; on every beat it
  is a flicker.
