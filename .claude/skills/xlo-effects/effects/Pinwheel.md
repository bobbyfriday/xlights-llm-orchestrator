# Pinwheel

The wheel on stars, spinners, wreaths and the matrix: almost always a counter-rotating pair, flipping direction or twist on every cell, and the most-curved effect after Spirals.

## Craft

### Identity and role

Arms radiating from a point and turning. The dials: **Arms** (1–8; 1 is a single sweeping
beam, 3–5 the classic wheel, 8 a fan); **Thickness** (10 a thin spoke, 30–46 a blade, 62 a
fat wheel with little gap); **Speed** (1–20; 4 slow, 10 default, 20 a spin); **Twist** (0 straight
spokes; ±150 curved blades like a turbine; sign = which way the curve leans); **Rotation** is a
checkbox that reverses the direction; **3D** is `3D` (shaded), `None` (flat), or `Sweep` (each
arm fades behind its leading edge, the radar look); **Arm Size** (100 fills the buffer, 200+
overflows it so only part of the wheel shows); **XC / YC** move the centre (−100…100; a corner
plus Arm Size 213 plus one arm is a searchlight); **Offset** turns the starting angle; **Style**
is `New Render Method` in 100% of the corpus.

Roles: **feature** on round props (spinners, stars, wreaths, snowflakes) and on the matrix;
**carrier** as 1–2 beat cells whose direction or twist flips every cell (Light of Christmas
spinner, Danger Zone star); **bed** as a slow Sweep at Speed 4 under everything for a whole
intro (Save Your Tears, 96 beats); and the thing beneath a **mask** (a Ripple ring or a Color
Wash with a reveal blend above it, 186 + 48 pairs). It stacks with itself more than any effect
but Spirals: 1,277 twins, almost all a counter-rotating pair (Rotation off on one layer, on on
the other, everything else identical). Used in 21 of 22 sequences: the most universal effect
after SingleStrand and Shockwave.

### Thematic fit

A pinwheel says *spinning*, *radiating*, *a wheel, a star, a sun, a snowflake turning, a
turbine*. On a star prop it is the star; on a spinner it is the spinner; on a wreath it is the
wreath. A single thick arm sweeping from a corner is a searchlight or a lighthouse. Eight thin
arms in Sweep are a fan or a radar. Twisted blades at Speed 20 are a jet engine (Danger Zone).
Any song about stars, wheels, sun, snow, turning, flying, searching, or a party wants it;
a lullaby wants it slow (Speed 4) and in Sweep, or not at all.

**How to permute.** Flip Rotation every cell (Danger Zone) or flip Twist's sign every beat
(Light of Christmas). Change Arms between sections (3 verse, 5 chorus). Change Speed between
phrases (1 then 4, Save Your Tears). Keep one color per layer and change the color on the
section. Move the centre only for a searchlight or a slide, never as a per-cell change.

### Value curves

| Want | Curve | Dial |
|---|---|---|
| blades curling one way then back across the phrase | Ramp Up/Down −360 → 360 → −360 | Twist |
| a twist settling | Ramp −122 → 122 | Twist |
| the wheel growing out of the prop | Ramp 0 → 100 (Arm Size or Thickness) | Arm Size, Thickness |
| a swell and release | Ramp Up/Down 0 → 100 → 0 | Thickness |
| the centre crossing the matrix | Ramp −100 → 100 | XC |
| a wobble | Sine (P2 100, P3 20) on XC, or Sine on Offset | XC, Offset |
| speeding up into the chorus | Ramp 10 → 30 | Speed |

Pinwheel is the most-curved effect in the corpus after Spirals: twist, thickness, arm size and
centre all move. `value_curves.MOTION_CURVES` already knows `("Pinwheel", "twist")` (spin) and
`("Pinwheel", "thickness")` (sweep).

### Prop fit and render style

Matrix 25%, stars 25%, spinners 16%, mega tree 14%, wreaths 6%, whole-house groups 5%,
snowflakes 4%. The default buffer on a single round prop; `Per Preview` with `Rotate CC 90` on
a star group so one wheel turns across all of them; `Per Model Per Preview` on spinner groups.
Rob's taste note: a Pinwheel on the mega tree reads odd; keep it to round props and the
matrix. Cheap to render.

### Timing

Cells of 1–2 beats (40%) with the direction or twist flipping per cell; 4–8 beats (34%) for
features and growing wheels; 16+ for beds in Sweep. Fades 0.10–0.25 on cells, 1–2 on beds.

### Anti-patterns

- **One Pinwheel alone, one direction, for a section.** 69% have a partner and the partner is
  usually the counter-rotating twin.
- **Pinwheel on the mega tree as the feature.** Rob: it reads odd; Spirals is the tree's wheel.
- **Three colors on a 3-arm wheel.** One color per layer; the pair makes the second color.
- **Speed 20 for eight beats.** 20 is a one-beat spin; a bed runs at 4.
- **Twist 0 all song.** The pros curl it (±150) and curve it; a straight wheel is the verse,
  not the show.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knob_values`: `Pinwheel#86` is the free look (Arms 1–6, Thickness 10–75, Speed
  1–25, Twist −154…168, XC/YC ±100, Arm Size 100–213, Offset 0–270, 3D choice); `#0` is the
  Danger Zone star wheel frozen; `#12` the flat wheel; `#3` carries the transform knob and
  `Sweep`; `#20` reaches Arms 8, Arm Size 400 and Twist 336.
- `effect_meta.py` maps `ltr` / `rtl` onto the Rotation checkbox and speed onto
  `E_SLIDER_Pinwheel_Speed` (5–20), class `cellable`, energy 2–5; `weave.counter_rotate_stacks`
  already counter-rotates a Pinwheel pair in a composite. The per-cell flip, the twist-sign
  alternation, the corner kaleidoscope and the Sweep bed are not synthesized; write them as
  `stack` layers (a counter pair is one layer with `twin: "counter"`).
