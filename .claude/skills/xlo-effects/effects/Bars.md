## Craft

### Identity and role

Straight bars of the palette colors moving across the buffer. **Direction** is the whole effect:
`up` / `down` / `Left` / `Right` sweep; `compress` / `expand` move toward or away from the
center (vertical); `H-compress` / `H-expand` do it horizontally; `Alternate Up` alternates.
**BarCount** is how many color bands share the height (1 = one band crossing, 4–5 = stripes);
**Cycles** is how many passes in the effect's length (0.5 = half a pass, 2 = two, 10–16 = a
shimmer); **3D** shades the bands, **Gradient** fades one color into the next, **Highlight**
adds a bright leading edge (the pros leave it off); **Center** shifts the compress point.

Roles: **carrier** first (a one-band bar rising on every beat is the cane and tree workhorse),
**feature** as a twin (compress + expand, or up + down with a transform), **mask** (Bars with
`2 is Unmask` cut a Shader, Spirals or Video texture into sweeping bars; Bars with `1 is Mask`
hide a bed in stripes), and **envelope** (Gradient bars with `Brightness` sweep a bed's
brightness from the center). Rarely a bed: BarCount 3–5 at Cycles 2 over 15 beats under a
Kaleidoscope is the exception.

### Thematic fit

Bars say *drive* and *direction*: a piston, a level meter, a curtain rising, a wipe. `up` on a
beat is lift; `down` is weight; `compress` on the beat is a clap; `H-expand` from the center is
an opening. They are the rock and funk rhythm effect (Danger Zone, Shut Up and Dance, Uptown
Funk) and the candy-cane tick in the Christmas files. A song with a straight eighth-note pulse,
a march, a stomp-clap, lyrics about rising, climbing, doors opening or walls falling wants them.
A waltz or a ballad does not.

**How to permute.** Keep the direction for a phrase and flip it on the next (Danger Zone: three
cells up, three cells down). Change the color pair every two bars (Light of Christmas canes:
green, blue, yellow, green). Between sections change the buffer: per-cane bars in the verse,
one bar across the group in the chorus. Between roles change the blend: the same `H-expand` bar
is a carrier at Normal, an envelope at Brightness, a texture cutter at `2 is Unmask`.

### Prop fit and render style

Mega tree 36%, canes 23%, whole-house groups 7%, small trees 5%, rooflines 4%, spinners 4%,
matrix 5%. Vertical directions on trees and canes; horizontal (`Left` / `Right` / `H-`) on
rooflines, arches and the matrix, or a vertical bar with `Rotate CW 90`. Per Model Per Preview
on cane and tree groups so each prop runs its own bar; Per Preview when one bar should cross the
whole group. Cheap to render anywhere.

### Timing

One beat (29%) and two beats (20%) as carriers; four to six beats for sweeps and envelopes;
fifteen to sixteen beats for twin textures and shimmers. Claps fade 0.10 both ways; stripe
phrases fade 0.5 in on the first cell and out on the last; everything else abuts at 0.

### Anti-patterns

- **Bars as a bed.** A long Bars at Cycles 1–2 with no re-cut is a slow wallpaper; the pros
  re-cut it every beat or two.
- **One direction all song.** Flip it every phrase; the corpus flips up/down and compress/expand
  constantly.
- **Highlight on.** Off in 100% of the corpus.
- **Hard bands over a textured bed at Normal.** Use Brightness (envelope) or `2 is Unmask`
  (texture bars); Normal over a bed just covers it.
- **BarCount 5 at Cycles 1 on a cane.** Five stripes on a short prop read as noise; one band.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knob_values`: `Bars#68` (the free one: Direction, BarCount 1–5, Cycles) covers
  the beat bar and the stripe phrase; `#21` / `#61` are the clap frozen exactly; `#58` has the
  H- directions and Gradient. `effect_meta.py` maps `ltr`/`rtl`/`up`/`down` onto
  `E_CHOICE_Bars_Direction` and speeds through `E_TEXTCTRL_Bars_Cycles` (0.5–4), so the beat
  bar and its direction flips are already reachable from a cell recipe.
- `extra_settings`: `T_CHOICE_LayerMethod` (`Brightness`, `2 is Unmask`, `1 is Mask`),
  `B_CHOICE_BufferTransform=Rotate CW 90`, `B_CHOICE_BufferStyle=Per Model Per Preview`, the
  buffer-rotation curve, `T_TEXTCTRL_Fadein/Fadeout`.
- Not synthesized anywhere: the Brightness sweep on section heads, texture bars over a Shader,
  the twin with transforms. Write them as `stack` layers (the `xlo-brief` skill).
