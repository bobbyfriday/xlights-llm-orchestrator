## Craft

### Identity and role

A single spiral arm that turns about (**Center X**, **Center Y**) — 50 / 50 in 81% or more;
Y 0 (19%, one sequencer) grows it from the bottom edge — starting at **Start Radius** /
**Start Width** / **Start Angle** and interpolating to **End Radius** / **End Width** over
**Duration** (20 in 84%, 9 seq / 6 songs: the move is done in the first fifth and the arm then
just turns; 0 in 3 seq / 3 songs; 60 once) while sweeping **Revolutions** degrees (1440, four
turns, in 86%, 8 seq / 6 songs; 720 is one sequencer's inward thirds). **Reverse** flips the
spin (23%, 3 seq / 2 songs); **Inward** draws the arm from the rim to the centre (14%, one
sequencer); **Blend Edges** softens the arm (on in 94%); **Accel** is 0 in 100%. The notebook
key (`E_NOTEBOOK_Galaxy` Start / Options / End) is the panel's last-open tab, not a setting;
ignore it. Three colours in 59% cycle along the arm; sparkles 28% (2 seq / 1 song).

The geometry makes three gestures. A **bloom**: Start 1 / 5 → End 224 / 180, 0 / 8 → 185 / 105,
0 / 5 → 209 / 255 (the arm grows out of the centre). A **collapse**: 250 / 255 → 0 / 0,
201 / 255 → 0 / 0, 57 / 30 → 0 / 0, 176 / 205 → 10 / 5 (the house is gathered into a point).
A **held turn**: 61 / 75 → 10 / 5 for 11–12 beats on a star, the arm thinning as it spins.

Roles: **feature** on whole-house groups (31%, 5 seq / 5 songs) and on the star (19%, one
sequencer, 12 placements); a 1–6 beat **gesture** on the house at a section head; a **twin** with
itself (20 pairs, 2 seq; identical only 10% — they differ by palette or by Reverse + a flip);
the thing beneath Warp (9, one sequence) and over Fan (6), Ripple `Layered` (3), Bars (3),
Spirals (9). Only 44% share the model: Galaxy is placed alone more than any other effect in
this group of files. Never a mask, never a carrier.

### Thematic fit

A spiral arm says *galaxy*, *nebula*, *whirlpool*, *portal*, *drain*, *hypnosis*. Held 11
beats at Duration 20 it is a slow hypnotic feature; the collapse (201 / 255 → 0 / 0) is the house
being pulled into one point, a pickup into a drop; the bloom from 0 is the hit itself. Christmas
is red + green + blue on the whole house (16 placements) and Greg Colburn's star turning in a
pink triad then a yellow triad every 11 beats, the star as an ornament. For Halloween, not from
the corpus but the corpus's own palettes point there: purple + orange + black on the hero matrix
is the portal (the corpus's `#000000+#80FFFF+#8000FF` already puts a black in the arm so it has
gaps); green + near-black (Shut Up and Dance's `#00FF00+#010101`) is a ghost swirl; a collapse on
the stakes before the drop is the graveyard being drained. Lyrics about spinning, falling,
stars, "round and round" want it.

### Value curves

None in 64 placements. The motion is the effect's own Start → End interpolation over
`Duration`, so a bloom or a collapse needs no curve. If a build needs the arm to grow across a
phrase instead of in its first fifth, set Duration 60 (one placement) or curve
`E_VALUECURVE_Galaxy_End_Radius` — not from the corpus. No `TT=` curve applies; `TT=Beats` works
only after `xlo finalize` has patched the Beats track.

### Anti-patterns

- **Galaxy as a 32-beat bed.** With Duration 20 the move is over in the first fifth; after that
  it is a turning arm. The longest corpus hold is 12 beats on a star.
- **Six colours.** The arm cycles them in bands and reads as confetti; three (59%).
- **The identical twin.** 10%; the corpus twin differs by palette or by Reverse + Flip.
- **Galaxy on a line with the default buffer.** A pulse, not a spiral; `Per Model Per Preview`
  or nothing.
- **Our pipeline: Galaxy as fabric.** It is in `MOTION_EFFECTS`, so the weaver may use it as a
  motion texture across energy 2–4 with `Reverse` as its only direction and the look's frozen
  geometry as its only shape, held as long as the section. Write the bloom / collapse / held turn
  as a stack layer with `cell_beats`.
- **Canvas on alone.** `#7` and `#8` carry `T_CHECKBOX_Canvas=1` from one sequencer; harmless
  by itself, useless without something beneath.
