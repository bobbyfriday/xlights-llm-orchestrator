## Craft

### Identity and role

A particle fluid simulation: up to four sources squirt particles into the buffer and physics
does the rest. From the corpus: **ParticleType** `Water` in 7 of 8 (one sequencer), `Elastic`
in Jeremy Poling's one; source 1 at **X1** 50, **Y1** 100 (top centre) with **Direction1** 270
(straight down), **Velocity1** 100, **Flow1** 100 (the Save Your Tears rain) or 7 (Jeremy's
trickle); **LifeTime** 328 frames (rain that dies before the floor) or 1000 (particles that
pile up); **Size** 699 (big drops) or 415; **Gravity** 10.0 everywhere; **HoldColor** on in all
8 (a particle keeps its colour); **MixColors**, **FlowMusic**, **Despeckle** 0, **WarmUpFrames**
0, **SourceSize** 0 everywhere; the barriers off except Jeremy's **BottomBarrier**, which with
his sources 2 and 4 (X 0 / 100, Y 50, Direction 0 / 180, Flow 13: the left and right edges
firing inward) makes a pool that fills from three sides. Not from the corpus, xLights'
behaviour: Direction is in degrees (0 right, 90 up, 180 left, 270 down), the barriers are walls
particles bounce off, Flow Music ties the flow to the audio level, Warm Up Frames pre-runs the
sim so it starts full, and the sim is render-heavy, worse with Size and Flow up and on a group
of many props. `E_NOTEBOOK1` is which source tab was open when saved; ignore it.

Role: **bed** on the matrix, 8 beats at a time, under a Video and over a Pinwheel ×4 (the AG
Save Your Tears stack, 6 of 8); once a 3-beat **feature** on the whole house under a Wave. 100%
share the model, 88% with three or more layers. Two sequences on two songs, 8 placements: one
sequencer's rain plus one experiment, and the rest is labelled.

### Thematic fit

Rain, a fountain, lava, blood, a cauldron. Cyan rain is Save Your Tears (6 of 8 placements, "a
tear in the rain"), a magenta gradient for its last verse. Halloween: red Water from the top of
the hero matrix (blood down the wall), orange + black Elastic with BottomBarrier on (a cauldron
filling), green Water for slime; purple particles from the left and right edges meeting in the
middle (Jeremy's three-source look) at a spell. Christmas: a white Water fall for "let it snow"
is Snowflakes' job; Liquid is for something that pours. Lyrics with rain, tears, pour, flow,
melt or drown get it literally. The sequencing skill keeps texture effects (Plasma, Fire,
Liquid) off line props.

### Prop fit and render style

Matrix 88% (one sequence, Matrix HD, the default buffer) and the whole house once at `Per Model
Per Preview`. A sim needs a 2D buffer and the render cost multiplies with every prop it runs on:
one Liquid on the hero matrix, not one per stake. On the Halloween layout: the hero seed
matrices, never the video panel, never `SEM_ALL` at Flow 100.

### Timing

8 beats 62%, 12 and 9 beats once each (median 4.05 s), 3 beats on the whole-house pool. Fade in
.2 (62%) so the first drops do not pop; fade out 1 on the last run. AG re-cuts the rain at the
same 8-beat length seven times through the song, always at a section head.

### Anti-patterns

- **Liquid on a line prop or `SEM_ALL`.** No height to fall through on a line; on the whole yard
  at Flow 100 the render crawls (`xlo-sequencing` keeps texture effects off line props).
- **Liquid alone on the matrix for a section.** AG never runs it without a Pinwheel beneath and
  the film above; a lone rain over black is a screensaver.
- **Re-cut per beat.** The sim restarts empty each cut (WarmUpFrames 0 in all 8); hold it 8
  beats.
- **Four colours with HoldColor and one source.** The drops come out in bands; one colour per
  source reads cleaner (AG: one colour).
- **Our own pipeline: `Liquid#0` from the picker.** The pool look bakes in `Per Model Per
  Preview` and three sources; on a group of many props that is the expensive case. Name `#2`
  in a stack.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knobs`: `Liquid#2` (the rain; `T_TEXTCTRL_Fadein` .2 / .5), `#1` (the rain, no
  fades), `#3` (the rain, fades .2 / 1), `#0` (the three-source pool, frozen). The picker
  rotates all four. A colour, Flow, LifeTime or Size off the look goes in `palette` and
  `settings` (`E_TEXTCTRL_Flow1`, `E_TEXTCTRL_LifeTime`, `E_TEXTCTRL_Size`, `E_TEXTCTRL_X1` /
  `_Y1`, `E_TEXTCTRL_Direction1`, `E_CHECKBOX_BottomBarrier`).
- `effect_meta`: energy band 2–3 only; no speed, direction or duration class, and not in
  `BED_EFFECTS`. Nothing synthesizes the rain stack: write a `stack` with a Pinwheel bed
  (`role: "bed"`), then Liquid `role: "feature"`, `look_id: "Liquid#2"`, `cell_beats: 0`,
  `fade_in: 0.2`, on the hero matrix group only.
