## Craft

### Identity and role

Concentric shapes growing out of (or shrinking into) a point: **Object** is the shape (Circle in
almost every placement; Square, Star with 5–6 points, Snow Flake on Christmas props);
**Movement** is `Explode` (out) or `Implode` (in); **Thickness** is the ring's width (3 = a thin
line, 6 = a clear ring, 100 = a filled shape that flashes); **Cycles** is how many rings cross in
the effect (1 = one ring per cell); **XC / YC** move the origin (XC 26 puts it off-center);
**3D** shades the ring. Newer builds add Rotation, Twist and Scale dials, curved in one
sequence.

The catalog and our own sequencing guide file Ripple under "gentle / ambient". The corpus does
not: 55% of placements are **one beat** and 14% two. Ripple is a **beat ring on radial props**
(spinners, stars, snowflakes, the matrix) and the community's favourite **mask**: a thin ring
with `1 is Unmask` / `1 is True Unmask` over a Pinwheel or Spirals reveals the feature in a
pulsing ring (186 pairs). Its bed role exists, but as a cascade: four thin rings on four layers
a tenth of a second apart, under a per-beat Spirals (Light of Christmas chorus Toby). Only 6
songs use it, so these are a few sequencers' habits, consistently applied.

### Thematic fit

A ring says *pulse*, *drop*, *echo*: a stone in water, a bell, a radar sweep, a heartbeat on a
star. Explode on the beat is a pulse outward; Implode on the off-beat answers it. Star and Snow
Flake shapes at Thickness 100 are literal ornaments flashing on a matrix. It belongs on round
props and on songs with a clear pulse or a water, echo, bell, or "ripple" lyric; it does not
belong on a line prop, where a ring is a dot.

**How to permute.** Alternate Explode and Implode every beat or every two. Move the origin
between sections (centre in the verse, XC 26 in the bridge). Thicken from 3 to 6 on the chorus.
Switch Normal to `1 is True Unmask` when a Pinwheel arrives beneath. Stack rings on staggered
layers for a burst on the drop. Keep one color per section; the ring is shape, not color.

### Prop fit and render style

Spinners 23%, matrix 20%, stars 15%, mega tree 12%, canes 11%, whole-house groups 10%,
snowflakes 4%. Round props first. On the matrix use Overlay - Scaled so the ring fills the
panel, or a SubBuffer quarter for four small rings. On a cane group use Per Model Default and
expect a dot pulse, which is fine as a tick.

### Timing

One beat (55%); two beats for Implode answers and reveal washes; four to six beats only for
2-cycle ornaments and bursts. Fades 0 unless named above.

### Anti-patterns

- **Ripple as a long ambient bed.** The corpus holds it for a beat and re-cuts. Four beats of one
  ring at Cycles 1 is a slow blink.
- **Ripple on a line prop.** A ring on a roofline is a dot.
- **Three colors.** One; the shape is the message. Palette lives in the Pinwheel beneath.
- **Explode all song.** Alternate with Implode.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knob_values`: `Ripple#47` (Explode/Implode, Thickness 3–70, XC 0–26, Cycles),
  `#11` (Overlay - Scaled), `#49` (Implode with `1 is True Unmask` as a knob option), `#26`
  (Square/Snow Flake with SubBuffer quarters and buffer rotation curves), `#30` (Star,
  Thickness to 100).
- `effect_meta.py` gives Ripple a speed key (`E_TEXTCTRL_Ripple_Cycles` 1–8), directions via
  `E_SLIDER_Ripple_Rotation` ±20, energy 2–3, `cellable`. The Explode/Implode alternation is not
  a known direction pair; map `center_out` / `center_in` onto `E_CHOICE_Ripple_Movement` next.
  Staggered bursts and the mask stacks are not synthesized anywhere; a
  `stack` layer with `blend: "1 is True Unmask"` over a Pinwheel layer is the ring window.
