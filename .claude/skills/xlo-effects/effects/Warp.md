## Craft

### Identity and role

Warp is not a picture; it is a distortion applied to **the layers beneath it**. It needs the
layer's **Canvas** box on (`T_CHECKBOX_Canvas=1`, frozen into every catalog look) so it renders
over the composite of everything below. **Type** picks the distortion: `mirror` (59%: the left
half of the buffer reflected onto the right, instant symmetry), `copy` (13%), `circular swirl`
and `banded swirl` (a vortex), `water drops` (ripples), `circle reveal`, `wavy`, `dissolve`.
**Treatment** is `constant` in 100% of the corpus (`in`, `out`, `in-out` animate the warp over
the effect; nobody uses them). **X / Y** place the warp's centre (50 / 50 in every placement,
either static or as a flat curve at 50). **Speed** (20, four sequences) and **Cycle Count** (1)
drive the swirls and drops; mirror ignores them.

Role: **overlay**, always on top. In the corpus it sits over four SingleStrand chases, four
Curtains, a Spirals twin, a Pinwheel pair, VU Meters, Garlands. It has no color of its own worth
choosing: the palette keys are saved but a mirror shows the colors beneath. Seven of 22 sequences
use it, five of them `mirror`; Light of Christmas alone places 100 of the 212.

### Thematic fit

`mirror` says *symmetry*: it turns any chase or ribbon into a kaleidoscope-lite, and it is how
the pros make a half-designed matrix look finished. `circular swirl` says *vortex*, *storm*,
*drain*; `water drops` says *rain*, *tears*, *a pond*; `dissolve` and `circle reveal` are
transitions. A chorus that wants to feel bigger than the verse without new effects gets a
mirror on top. A song about storms, water, mirrors, dreams or vertigo gets the swirl or the
drops literally. A ballad on a cane group gets nothing: Warp needs a 2D buffer.

**How to permute.** Do not permute the warp; permute what is under it. A mirror over a
one-direction chase makes a chevron; over a counter-rotating Spirals pair it makes a lattice;
over Curtains it makes a closing iris. Add the spinning-mirror curve on the final chorus.
Switch mirror to swirl for one bridge and back.

### Prop fit and render style

Matrix 56%, mega tree 33%, whole-house groups 4%, rooflines 3%. Needs a 2D buffer with width:
a mirror on a line prop reflects nothing visible. The default buffer in 98% of placements.
Render cost is moderate on a big matrix; keep one Warp per model.

### Timing

Fades 0.10 (mirror) or 0.25 (spinning mirror, copy).

### Anti-patterns

- **Canvas off.** The warp then distorts nothing and shows an empty layer. Every catalog look
  freezes `T_CHECKBOX_Canvas=1`; keep it.
- **Warp with nothing beneath it.** It is an overlay; alone it is black.
- **Treatment in/out on a mirror.** Unused by the pros; the mirror pops in at a fade of 0.10.
- **Moving X / Y per cell.** Never done; the centre stays at 50 / 50.
- **Our own use: never.** 173 pairs with Spirals and 194 with SingleStrand in the corpus; zero
  Warp placements in our shows. It is the cheapest "make the chorus bigger" there is.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knob_values`: `Warp#27` (mirror, fades as knobs), `#23` (mirror with the flat
  X/Y curves), `#13` (mirror with buffer rotation), `#16` (circular swirl, Speed 20), `#11`
  (copy). All freeze `T_CHECKBOX_Canvas=1` and `Warp_Treatment_APPLYLAST=constant`.
- `effect_meta.py` has only a speed key (`E_SLIDER_Warp_Speed` 5–30) and no class; nothing
  places Warp as an overlay on top of a stack, and the layer clamp would count it as one of four.
  Write it as the top `stack` layer with `role: "overlay"` and `look_id: "Warp#27"`; the
  layers beneath it in the same stack are what it mirrors.
