## Craft

### Identity and role

Shader renders a `.fs` file (`E_0FILEPICKERCTRL_IFS`) on the buffer. The file's own uniforms
appear as `SHADERXYZZY_<name>` dials and differ per file; the common dials are **Speed**
(−373…800; 100 is normal, negative runs the shader backwards), **Zoom** (−74…100), **Offset X /
Y** and **LeadIn**. The files the corpus uses, each one sequencer's and each stored at an
absolute path on that sequencer's disk:

- **Vortex V2.fs** (41%, Believer): ColorB 0, ColorG 0, Filling 296, Half 0, Rotation 50,
  Stripe 2900 — a spinning vortex in the palette's red, Speed ±302.
- **Black Cherry Cosmos.fs** (12% under the Believer path; the same file under two other paths in
  Uptown Funk and Sounding Joy, so three sequences by one sequencer): mouseX / mouseY 0, Speed
  ±100, Zoom 0 to −74, white + black or red.
- **Spiral over Beams.fs** (8%, Uptown Funk and Believer): uColMode "Alternate Color Palette (3
  used)", uCore 26, uIntensity 64, uZoom 145 (house) or 861 (tree), Speed ±52, orange.
- **Blue Streaks_N_1.fs** and **Fly Through_NN.fs** (Shut Up and Dance, Bill Jenkins): uContRot
  on, uIntensity 100, uZoom 500, uOffsetY −10; j 50 k 50 — re-saved with a running number per
  placement.
- **Hex Vortex.fs** (4%, Magic): R 5 G 12 B 25, shapeX 200 shapeY 225, rotation 5, speed 50.
- **Continua Variation.fs** (4%), **Light Show Shader.fs** (Uptown Funk), **p6mmInversion.fs**
  (CX 200 CY −200, Light of Christmas Bill Jenkins), **RainbowGlowRing.fs** (Light of Christmas
  XATW), **InnerDimensionalMatrix.fs** (Danger Zone).

Roles: **texture under a stamp** first — 83% of placements have a partner and the partner
above is an On `2 is Unmask` (243), a VU Meter `2 is True Unmask` (109) or a Bars `2 is Unmask`
(82): the shader moves, the stamp gives it a flat color on the beat. **Overlay** as the second
Shader over an On at `1 reveals 2` (119) or `Additive` (63) — the Believer sandwich. **Bed** on
its own for a verse (Black Cherry Cosmos, 30 beats, 153 bare placements). Not a carrier: the
beat comes from the stamp. Half the placements are four beats.

### Thematic fit

A vortex says *spin* and *pull*; the cosmos says *stars*; Spiral over Beams says *searchlights*;
Blue Streaks says *speed*. It is the arena look of the pop and rock files (Believer, Uptown
Funk, Shut Up and Dance) on the mega tree (47%, 7 seq / 7 songs) and the whole house (21%,
11 / 9). One color 69% (red 457 is Believer's vortex, orange 72 is Uptown Funk's beams), white
+ black 99, the 8-color rainbow 71 under VU stamps. Halloween: a vortex in orange or purple on
the hero tree with an orange stamp above; the cosmos in white + black as the night sky under
the verse. Christmas: Hex Vortex and RainbowGlowRing on the tree.

### Anti-patterns

- **A shader alone on a group for a section.** A screensaver; 83% have a partner. Put the stamp
  above it.
- **Seven colors on a file that reads one.** Vortex V2 takes the first color; only Spiral over
  Beams ("Alternate Color Palette (3 used)") reads three.
- **Speed 302 that never flips.** Believer flips every 4 beats.
- **A shader on a line prop** or a single strand.
- **A fast shader Per Model on a group of many small props.** The render cost multiplies.

### What code can realize today

- **Nothing places a Shader.** It is in `ASSET_BOUND_TYPES` (`xlights_core/knowledge/constants.py`:
  Faces, Pictures, Video, Shader, DMX) and has no catalog looks (`pick_pool: []`), so
  `stack.expand_stack` drops a layer whose type has no ranked looks (`_layer_plan`); the
  emitter's asset-bound branch knows only Faces (`effect_emitter._ASSET_BOUND`) and the
  direct-settings allowlist only Text and Faces (`DIRECT_TYPES`, Shader deliberately out).
- **The path that would work** is the one `pipeline/matrix_video.py` takes for Video: a per-song
  JSON naming the target and the file, a settings string built in code with
  `serialize_settings` — `E_0FILEPICKERCTRL_IFS=<path>`, the `Speed` slider, the `Zoom` /
  `Offset_X` / `Offset_Y` / `LeadIn` text controls and the file's `SHADERXYZZY_*` uniforms —
  emitted as one `EffectInstruction` with `look_id=""`, plus "Shader" in the emitter's
  asset-bound set so it places from explicit settings. The `.fs` files must sit in the show
  folder where the sandboxed xLights can read them; the corpus paths are other people's disks.
- **Until then**, write the stamp recipes over a placeable texture — Spirals, Pinwheel,
  Kaleidoscope, Butterfly — with the On `2 is Unmask` or VU `2 is True Unmask` layer above; that
  layer is placeable today and does the same job.
- `effect_meta.py` has no Shader row (no energy band, speed or direction); `blend`, `transform`
  and the fades would carry over as fields, and the Speed / Zoom curves as `settings`, once a
  placement path exists.
