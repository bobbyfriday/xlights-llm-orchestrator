# Snowflakes

Falling flakes held for a phrase or a verse on the mega tree, the matrix and the whole house: white, mostly `Driving`, often twinned with a second flake `Type`, never curved; a Christmas effect that only becomes ash or rising embers by palette and a `Rotate 180`.

## Craft

### Identity and role

Flake sprites falling through the buffer. **Falling** is the motion: `Driving` (55%, 8 seq /
4 songs: the flakes drift as they fall), `Falling` (32%, 3 seq / 3 songs: straight down),
`Falling & Accumulating` (14%, 2 seq / 2 songs: they pile up at the bottom). **Count** is the
density (6 in 21%, 2 seq / 2 songs; 5 in 2 seq / 2 songs; 15 one sequencer's; 50–55 in 2 seq /
2 songs each; 100 in 3 seq / 2 songs is a whiteout; range 4–100). **Speed** is 10 in 66%
(9 seq / 5 songs); 8 is one sequencer's drift; 20 (2 seq / 2 songs) to 25 a storm. **Type**
0–9 picks the flake drawing: 1 in 44% (5 seq / 5 songs), 2 (3 seq / 3 songs), 9 (2 seq /
2 songs), 5 (3 seq / 2 songs); not from the corpus: 0 is a single pixel and the high Types are
the big drawn six-armed flakes, so Type is the size dial as much as the shape dial. **Warmup
Frames** is absent in 82%; 20 once, so the field is full on the downbeat. Not one value curve
in 73 placements: this effect is set and held.

Roles: **feature** on the mega tree (36%, 7 seq / 4 songs) and the matrix (26%, 5 seq /
3 songs) for a phrase or a verse, with a Shape, Pinwheel or Spirals above it; **bed** on the
whole house (23%, 6 seq / 5 songs; the Magic 82-beat fall); a **twin** with itself (22 pairs,
4 seq: two layers differing by Type, identical in 45%). Never a carrier (the 2-beat cells are
10%, one habit), never a mask; the one `Layered` Count 100 (2 placements) is the nearest thing
to an accent. 70% share the model (10 seq / 5 songs). Eleven sequences but six songs, and all
but four placements are the Christmas files: in use, this is a Christmas effect.

### Thematic fit

It says *snow*, and the corpus takes it literally: white in 31 of 73 placements, white + blue
(9), pale cyan + white (5), pink + cyan on Kid On Christmas (4), white + black (3) on Magic.
Two colours in 37%: the flakes alternate colour, not the field. Flakes need a lyric or a season
("let it snow", a verse about winter) more than any other effect here; on a dance track they are
the Christmas garnish, placed 2 beats at a time.

Halloween inversions, by palette and transform, not from the corpus: Type 0–1 at Count 6,
Speed 8, grey + black is **ash** drifting down the house; orange + red Type 1 is **embers**; and
`Rotate 180` (2 seq / 2 songs in the corpus; Magic ran its whole-house `Falling` under it for 82
beats) turns the fall into a **rise**, so orange + black Falling Count 41 Rotate 180 is embers
rising off the hero. A white Type 5–9 flake on a Halloween show reads as Christmas; keep the
drawing small and the palette warm or sick-green.

**How to permute.** Change the Type between the twin layers and between sections, not the
Speed (10 in two thirds of the corpus). `Driving` in the verse, `Falling & Accumulating` at the
end of a section so the pile is the settle. Move Count with the prop: 6 on a tree, 15 for the
twin, 41–55 on the yard, 100 only as a 2-beat hit. `Per Preview` for the yard, the default
buffer for the tree. Give the second occurrence a second colour (37%) or a `Rotate 180`.

### Value curves

None. Zero of 73 placements curve anything; the pros set Count and Speed and hold them. If a
build needs the field to thicken, curve `E_VALUECURVE_Snowflakes_Count` (a Ramp, as Meteors
does with its Count) — not from the corpus. No `TT=` curve applies; a `TT=Beats` curve would
work only after `xlo finalize` has patched the Beats track.

### Prop fit and render style

Mega tree 36% (7 seq / 4 songs), matrix 26% (5 seq / 3 songs), whole-house groups 23% (6 seq /
5 songs); flakes, arches, mini trees and spinners 3% each (2 seq); rooflines, canes and
icicles once. The flakes need height: on a tree they fall down the cone, on a matrix they are
sprites, on a line they are dots passing. The default buffer on a tree or matrix. `Per Preview`
(2 seq / 2 songs) so one snowfall crosses the whole yard and every prop catches its share, the
better whole-house snow; `Per Model Default` (2 seq / 2 songs) so each prop has its own; `Horizontal
Stack` (one sequencer) on a tree group so the trees are one wide sky. On the Halloween layout:
the feature on the hero matrix, `Per Preview` ash across the stakes (a stake is a column and the
flakes run down it), nothing on the cats, where a flake is a blink.

### Anti-patterns

- **Speed 25 on a chorus.** The corpus holds Speed 10 at every energy; a storm is Count, not
  Speed. Our pipeline maps intensity onto `E_SLIDER_Snowflakes_Speed` 10–25, so a loud section
  gets a blizzard rush nobody wrote: pin Speed in `knobs`.
- **Count 100 for a verse.** A whiteout is a 2-placement hit; verses are Count 6–15.
- **The identical twin.** 45% of corpus twins; the other half differ by Type, which is the point.
- **Three colours.** 1%; one or two, and the second colour alternates per flake.
- **Snowflakes on a roofline, cane or cat.** Dots passing; one sequence each and never again.
- **A 1-beat flake cell.** The field never builds; 2 beats is the shortest the corpus uses and
  that is a garnish.
- **White Type 9 flakes on a Halloween section.** It says Christmas however the song goes.
