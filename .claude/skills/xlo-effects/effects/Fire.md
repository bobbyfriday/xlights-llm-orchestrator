## Craft

### Identity and role

Flames licking up from one edge of the buffer. From the corpus: **Location** is `Bottom` in all
8 placements (xLights also offers Top, Left and Right; not seen); **Height** is the flame reach
(19 on a whole-house group, 50 on mini trees, 55 scaled over a whole display; absent when the
music drives it); **Hue Shift** rotates the flame colours (0 and 10 for a natural fire; 79 in
Eliot Wisser's four placements, which with his pink / cyan / purple palette reads as cold fire);
**Grow With Music** (on in 2 placements, the Danger Zone stars) lets the audio level set the
height; **Growth Cycles** (4.0, 2.5, 0.0) is how many times the flames swell and settle over the
effect when the music is not driving them (0 = steady). Not from the corpus, xLights' behaviour:
the flame ramp is generated (red → orange → yellow at Hue Shift 0) and Hue Shift 0–100 walks it
round the hue wheel (79 lands in violet, which matches Eliot's palette; green sits near the
middle of the range); whether the palette tints the ramp depends on the build, so treat Hue Shift
as the colour dial, set the palette to match it, and check the preview. The flames are random per
frame, so Fire never looks the same twice and needs no re-cutting.

Role: **bed**. Seven of eight placements are alone on the model; the one pair is Fire over an
`On`. Nothing in the corpus sits above it. Four of 22 sequences on two songs: too thin for
community habits, so what follows leans on xLights' behaviour and Rob's Halloween brief and is
labelled where it does.

### Thematic fit

Fire says *heat*, *danger*, *hell*, *a hearth*, *afterburner*. Halloween: it is the answer to
Christmas's snow, the bed for a graveyard (flames behind the tombstone stakes), a cauldron on the
hero matrix, the ground burning under the cats. Orange at Hue Shift 0; violet around 79 (Eliot's
cold fire, with a purple palette) for a witch's fire; green near the middle of the range (not
from the corpus; the one green corpus placement is Jeremy Poling's Height 19 fire at Hue Shift 0
with a green palette, so check whether your build takes the palette). Christmas: a hearth on the
house in a warm song; red / orange / yellow (the Danger Zone palette) on the stars. Lyrics with
burn, fire, hot, hell or candle get it literally. Grow With Music on a loud chorus; Growth Cycles
2.5–4 in a verse so the flames breathe without the music.

**How to permute.** Height by section (19 in the verse, 50 in the chorus, Grow With Music on the
peak); Hue Shift by palette, never mid-section; Growth Cycles 4 in a slow section, 0 under a busy
stack; `Per Model Default` on the stakes, `Per Preview` on an ensemble, the default buffer on the
matrix.

### Layering recipes

The corpus has one pair (Fire over an `On`, Normal). The rest is not from the corpus, built from
the blend semantics in SKILL.md:

| Name | Stack (top → bottom) | Blend | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire over a lit bed** | Fire · On | Normal | the black above the flames is transparent, so the On shows over them (the one corpus pair) |
| **Flames under a thin feature** | Spirals (thin) or Fan (Blade Width 36–50) · Fire | Normal | the feature's gaps show the fire; a solid feature covers it (not from the corpus) |
| **Fire mandala** | Kaleidoscope `#0` · Fire | Normal | the flames tiled into a burning rune on the matrix (not from the corpus) |
| **Fire as brightness** | Fire `Brightness` · Color Wash | Brightness | a wash that flickers like firelight: the flames become the wash's brightness envelope (not from the corpus) |
| **Fire reveal** | Fire `1 is Unmask` · a feature | `1 is Unmask` | the feature shows only where the flames reach (not from the corpus) |

### Value curves

No Fire placement in the corpus carries a value curve. Not from the corpus: a Height curve
follows the same grammar as every other curve in these files, for example a build
`Active=TRUE|Id=ID_VALUECURVE_Fire_Height|Type=Ramp|Min=0.00|Max=100.00|P1=19.00|P2=60.00|RV=TRUE|`
under the key `E_VALUECURVE_Fire_Height`; verify it in xLights before relying on it, and prefer
Grow With Music for the music-driven case, which the corpus shows working. No timing-track
curves; a `TT=Beats` curve works only after `xlo finalize` has patched the Beats track.

### Prop fit and render style

The corpus: whole-house groups (2 seq / 2 songs), stars, mini trees, a spinner, pixel stakes,
arches, one each. Fire needs height in the buffer: on a tree, a matrix, a star or a stake it
rises; on a roofline with the default buffer it is a one-row flicker. On the Halloween layout:
`Per Model Default` on the stake group so each tombstone burns; the default buffer on the hero
matrix for one big fire at Height 50–55; `Per Preview` on the whole yard for one fire that climbs
every prop by its position (Eliot's buffer on the ensembles). Keep it off the video panel, and
off the cats unless the song wants a burning cat.

### Timing

8 beats 57% (4 placements), 31 beats 29%, 4 beats 14%; median 7 s. It holds a phrase or a
section and is never re-cut; the flames move by themselves. Fades .5 / .5 (Eliot), 1 / 1 (two
sequences), 3 / 1 on the music fire: a slow catch and a quick snuff.

### Anti-patterns

- **Fire on a line prop or an arch with the default buffer.** No height, no flames.
- **Hue Shift mid-section.** The colour of the fire is the section's identity; change it at the
  boundary.
- **Fire re-cut per beat.** It is random per frame already; a cut restarts the flames and reads
  as a strobe.
- **A solid feature on top.** Blade Width 100 or solid Bars cover the fire; keep the feature thin
  or put the fire on top as a `Brightness` envelope.
- **Grow With Music in a quiet verse.** The flames die; use Growth Cycles.
- **Our own pipeline: Fire as a motion carrier.** `MOTION_EFFECTS` includes Fire, so the weave
  can cell it like a chase; a Fire cut every 2 beats flickers. In a stack give it `cell_beats: 0`.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knobs`: `Fire#1` (`E_SLIDER_Fire_Height` 19–50, `E_SLIDER_Fire_HueShift` 0–79,
  `E_TEXTCTRL_Fire_GrowthCycles` 0.0 / 4.0, `B_CHOICE_BufferStyle` Per Model Default / Per
  Preview, fades .5 / 1; Grow With Music frozen off); `#2` (cold fire, frozen); `#3` (Grow With
  Music frozen on, Hue Shift 10, fades 3 / 1); `#0` (Overlay - Scaled, Height 55, Growth Cycles
  2.5). A Height above 50 or a Hue Shift between the observed values goes in `settings` as the raw
  key; Grow With Music with another hue is `#3` plus `E_SLIDER_Fire_HueShift` in `settings`.
- `effect_meta` gives Fire an energy band (2–4) and membership in `MOTION_EFFECTS`; no speed,
  direction or duration class. Nothing synthesizes a flame bed, a Height curve or the stake fire:
  write a `stack` layer with `role: "bed"`, `look_id: "Fire#1"`, `render_style: "Per Model
  Default"` on the stakes, and a thin feature above it.
