## Craft

### Identity and role

A fill of the palette colors across the buffer, cycling through them over the effect. The
dials: **Cycles** (how many times the palette cycles; 1 or 2; absent = one), **HFade** and
**VFade** (fade the fill toward the horizontal or vertical edges; both on = a soft radial pool
in the middle), **ReverseFades** (fade from the centre outward instead, lighting the edges),
**CircularPalette** (cycle smoothly round the palette instead of stepping), **Shimmer** (a
flicker). With every box off it is a plain fill: the model turns one color, then the next.

The catalog and our pipeline treat Color Wash as the ambient bed. The corpus uses it as two
things: a **beat pulse on the flood lights** (29% of placements, 6 sequences: one color per
beat, fading out 0.25, color + black so it dies to dark), and a **gradient pool on a matrix or
star** (HFade + VFade, one or two beats, re-cut with the beat). The bed role exists, as four
Color Wash layers stacked with different colors, or a 4-beat CircularPalette cycle with soft
fades, but it is the minority. Half of all placements carry sparkles.

### Thematic fit

A wash says *mood* and *room tone*: the color of the moment with no shape. On floods it is the
wall of the house taking the song's color on every beat. On a matrix with fades it is a glow
pooled in the middle. Two colors alternating on the beat (pink / cyan in Kid On Christmas,
purple / green in Shut Up and Dance) is the pop idiom; yellow dying to black every two beats is
a Christmas verse. It fits every song as the floor, and fits as the only effect on a prop in
intros, bridges and the last line.

section. Lengthen the fade out at phrase ends. Turn the pool into an edge glow when a feature
arrives in the middle. Move from floods only (verse) to floods + matrix + stars (chorus).

### Prop fit and render style

Flood lights 29% (six sequences), matrix 15%, stars 10%, mega tree 10%, snowflakes 7%,
rooflines 7%, arches 6%, icicles 5%. Per Model Default on flood groups so each flood takes its
own cell; the default buffer on a matrix for pools. It is the only effect in the corpus that is
mostly placed on floods.

### Timing

One beat 59%, two beats 13%; 4 beats for CircularPalette cycles; 8–16 beats for held beds.
Fade out 0.25 on pulses, 0.20 / 0.20 on two-beat cells, 1 at phrase ends; 0 on stutters.

### Anti-patterns

- **Color Wash as the only bed for a whole section at Normal with no fades.** A flat fill is a
  flat fill; use HFade + VFade for a pool, or re-cut it on the beat.
- **Three or four colors in one wash.** It steps through them and reads as a malfunction. Two,
  swapped per beat.
- **Ignoring the floods.** 29% of the community's washes are on floods; ours never target them
  because the layout has no flood group yet.
- **Sparkles off.** Half the corpus sparkles its washes; a sparkle-free wash reads dead on a
  matrix.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knob_values`: `#76` (HFade + ReverseFades), `#55` (HFade + VFade, SubBuffer
  quarters), `#19` / `#36` (Per Model Default with fade out), `#0` (plain). `effect_meta.py` has
  a speed key (`E_TEXTCTRL_ColorWash_Cycles` 1–6) and `bed_capable`; the weaver already uses
  Color Wash as a bed.
- `extra_settings`: `E_CHECKBOX_ColorWash_CircularPalette=1`, `E_CHECKBOX_ColorWash_Shimmer=1`,
  `C_SLIDER_SparkleFrequency`, `T_TEXTCTRL_Fadeout`, `B_CHOICE_BufferTransform=Flip Horizontal`.
- Not synthesized: the per-beat flood pulse with swapped colors, the pool re-cut with the beat,
  stacked washes. A flood group in the layout is the prerequisite for the first.
