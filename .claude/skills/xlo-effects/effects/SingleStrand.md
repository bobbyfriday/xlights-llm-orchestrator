## Craft

### Identity and role

A chase: a run of lit pixels with a tail travelling along the strand. It is the single most
placed effect in the corpus (13,636 placements, 20 of 22 sequences) and the house-outline, arch
and cane workhorse. The dials that matter: **Chase Type** (`Left-Right`, `Right-Left`, `From
Middle`, `To Middle`, the four `Bounce` variants) is the direction; **Color Mix** (1–100) is the
chase's length as a share of the strand (10 = a short comet, 33–50 = a third to half the strand
lit, 100 = the whole strand lit with a fading tail); **3D Fade** gives the tail a gradient;
**Fade Type** `From Head` makes the tail fade away behind the head; **Number of Chases** (1–20)
puts several comets on the strand; **Rotations** is how many passes in the effect's length
(1.0 = one pass); **Group All** chases the whole group as one strand instead of each model.

The `FX` notebook (Fireworks 1D, Bpm, Meteor…) and the `Skips` page exist in the settings of
almost every placement because xLights saves every tab, but `SSEFFECT_TYPE` is `Chase` in all
but 3%; ignore the FX keys.

Roles: **carrier** above all (a one-beat chase re-cut every beat on outlines, arches and canes),
**feature** as crossing twins (two layers in opposite directions, 3,552 pairs), **mask source**
(an On layer above with `2 is Unmask` gives a hard flat-color comet, 785 pairs), and **bed**
only as a whole-strand fade (Color Mix 100 with 3D Fade, 2 beats). Never an accent: the hit goes
on top of it.

### Thematic fit

A chase says *travel* and *pulse*: light running along the eaves, around an arch, up a cane. It
is the most literal rhythm effect there is, which is why it is 74% single-color and 33% white:
it marks time without saying anything else. `From Middle` / `To Middle` alternating every beat
is a heartbeat; `Left-Right` then `Right-Left` is a scan; two crossing chases are traffic. Any
song with a steady pulse wants it on the line props. Anything about running, driving, trains,
racing, chasing or a journey wants it literally.

**How to permute.** Alternate the direction every beat or every bar (`Left-Right` then
`Right-Left`; `From Middle` then `To Middle`). Lengthen the comet as the section fills (Mix 10
in the verse, 25 in the pre-chorus, 50 in the chorus, 100 for the phrase-end sweep). Keep one
color per section and change it on the section. Move the layer every four beats so twins cross
rather than stack. Change the buffer between props, not within a section.

### Prop fit and render style

Arches 21%, rooflines and outlines 17%, canes 15%, small trees 10%, stars 8%, matrix 6%, mega
tree 6%. `Per Model Per Preview` for arch and cane groups (each prop runs its own chase in
preview space), `Per Preview` for one chase along the whole outline, `Per Model Single Line` with
a 90° transform for canes and columns. Cheap anywhere; a swarm of ten comets on a long outline
is still cheap.

### Timing

One beat 54%, two beats 29%: re-cut on every beat, direction alternating. Two-beat Mix 100
sweeps at phrase ends. Four to eight beats only with Number of Chases 5+ or the fill curve.
Fades 0 for comets, .5 out for sweeps.

### Anti-patterns

- **One direction all section.** The corpus alternates on every beat. A chase that never turns
  is a conveyor belt.
- **Multi-color comets.** 74% are one color; the palette lives in the bed beneath. Three colors
  on a ten-pixel comet is confetti.
- **Long chases with Rotations 1.** A four-beat chase at one rotation is a slow crawl; either
  re-cut per beat or raise Rotations.
- **The whole strand lit (Mix 100) as a carrier.** It is the phrase-end sweep, not the beat.
- **Trusting the FX keys.** Fireworks 1D in the settings is a saved tab, not the effect.
