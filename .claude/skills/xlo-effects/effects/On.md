# On

## Craft

### Identity and role

On lights every pixel of the buffer in one color (or the palette as a gradient) and holds it.
Four dials shape the hold: **Start intensity → End intensity** (`E_TEXTCTRL_Eff_On_Start` /
`E_TEXTCTRL_Eff_On_End`, 0–100) ramp the brightness across the placement, so End 0 is a pop
that decays to black and Start 0 is a swell; **Cycles** (`E_TEXTCTRL_On_Cycles`) repeats that
ramp (2 = two pulses in one placement); **Shimmer** (`E_CHECKBOX_On_Shimmer`) flickers the
fill on alternate frames; **Transparency** (`E_TEXTCTRL_On_Transparency`, 13–34 in one 2024
file) lets the layer beneath through. On has no shape and no motion: everything an On *looks
like* comes from its color, its length, and the blend between it and the layer under it.

The corpus uses it four ways, and the most-used one is not the bed:

- **Carrier**: a one-beat pop (36% of placements are one beat) re-cut on every beat, with End
  0 so each pop decays (1,063 placements, 8 seq / 7 songs). Light of Christmas chases
  red → green → blue across the mini trees this way for a whole verse.
- **Accent**: the flood flash, Start 100 → End 0, `Additive`, white + black (floods are 14% of
  placements, 14 seq / 10 songs).
- **Mask source**: `2 is Unmask` is the blend on 36% of placements (5 seq / 5 songs, 1,993):
  an On one layer above a textured effect shows only where that effect is lit, so the
  Shockwave ring (475 pairs), the Shader vortex (511), the Spirals (238), the Twinkle (219),
  the Wave (81) or the Video (62) comes out in On's flat color with a hard edge. `Brightness`
  (3 seq / 3 songs) is the other mask use: the On's ramp becomes the brightness envelope of a
  SingleStrand, Morph or Color Wash beneath it.
- **Bed**: the long plain On (4+ beats, a quarter of placements) on the whole display, the
  roofline or the trees under a feature. This is what our pipeline mostly makes of it.

It never carries a section alone on a group canvas: a flat panel of color is the samey look.

### Thematic fit

On says nothing by itself; its color and its timing say everything. A one-beat white On is a
hit; red/green/blue per beat on the trees is the Christmas chase (red 951, green 832, blue 796
placements — the three most-used palettes); a long dim orange fill is a lit house between
features. Orange + black (120) is already the eighth palette in the corpus, so the Halloween
reading is native: an orange or purple bed on `SEM_ALL` under the feature, a one-beat white
pop on the stakes as the hit, an orange On `2 is Unmask` over a Shockwave or a Spirals for a
hard-edged shape. One color 78% of the time, two (white + black for the Additive flash) 16%;
three colors on an On is a striped gradient and the pros reach for it only as a bed (382
gradient placements).

**How to permute.** Change what the pop *does*, not the pop: color per beat in the verse, per
bar in the chorus; End 0 in the verse, End 20 in the chorus so the prop keeps a floor. Move On
between roles across sections — bed in the verse, flat stamp over the chorus feature, flood
flash on the drop — rather than between looks. Change the blend before the color. For a twin,
Believer offsets the second flash by 0.15 s on the layer above; Magic changes the stamp's color
every 2–3 beats while the texture beneath never changes.

### Prop fit and render style

Mini trees 22% (13 seq / 10 songs), floods 14% (14 / 10), mega tree 13% (17 / 11), stars 12%
(7 / 5), whole-display groups 6% (16 / 10), canes 5%, roofline 5% (15 / 10), arches 4% (17 /

seed matrices for stamps over a Spirals or a Shockwave. Never a bare On on the hero matrix.

### Timing

One beat 36%, two 16%, four 16%, three 10%, eight 5%. Pops are one beat, re-cut 20+ in a row.
Stamps run 2–8 beats with the color changing every 2–3 beats (Magic). Beds hold the section
with fades of 1 s. Flood flashes are 1–2 beats, the twin 0.15 s ahead. Transitions off.

### Anti-patterns

- **A plain On as the only effect on a group for a section.** A flat color panel — our bed when
  the weave has nothing to put over it. Put a texture under it or a stamp over it.
- **A one-beat white On as the hit.** We did it 42% of the time; the community rings instead
  (Shockwave.md). If it must be flat, stamp it through a ring with `2 is Unmask`.
- **Cycles on a bed.** The whole house pulses; `effect_meta` deliberately gives On no speed row.
  The double pulse is a 1–2 beat gesture (`On#9`), never a section.
- **Three colors on a one-beat On.** Stripes. One color; white + black only for Additive flashes.
- **The same first look every time.** The picker rotates `On#0`, `#31`, `#1`, `#9`, `#58`, `#27` —
  note that `#9` (Cycles 2) and `#27` (Shimmer) both pulse, so a bed taken from the pool can
  flicker. Name `On#0` for a bed and `On#31` for pops.
- **Shimmer as a default.** 1% of the corpus.

- `settings`: `E_TEXTCTRL_Eff_On_Start` / `_End` when the look does not expose them (extra
  settings are merged after the look's knobs at placement, so they win), `E_TEXTCTRL_On_Cycles`,
  `E_CHECKBOX_On_Shimmer`, `B_SLIDER_Blur`, the transition keys and the curve strings above;
  `brightness` is a field (code writes the brightness setting), as are `fade_in` / `fade_out`,
  `transform` and `render_style`.
- `effect_meta.py`: On is `bed_capable`, energy 1–5, no speed, no direction, duration free. Code
  synthesizes its own Ons: the whole-display beds (`peak_fill`, `ensemble_bed`, `_dim_bed`), the
  white `key_moment_flashes` on `SEM_ALL`, and `_accent_look` falls back to On. The flat stamp,
  the envelope, the flood twin and the Off gate are not synthesized: a stack layer with
  `blend: "2 is Unmask"` directly above the textured layer is the stamp; the 0.15 s twin offset
  has no field (use two layers, or `twin: "color"` for the color answer).
