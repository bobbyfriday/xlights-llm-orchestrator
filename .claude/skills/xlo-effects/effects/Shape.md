## Craft

### Identity and role

Shape draws **Count** copies of one **ObjectToDraw** (Circle, Square, Triangle, Star with
**Points**, Heart, Snowflake, Candy Cane, Tree, Present, Crucifix, an Emoji by **Char**, or an
SVG file) and lets each live for **Lifetime** frames, growing by **Growth** per frame (negative
shrinks), born at **StartSize** with an outline **Thickness** (100 = filled). **Random Location**
puts each new shape somewhere new and **Random Initial** scatters the starting ages so the field
is busy from frame one: together they are the firework mode. With both off, **Centre X / Y**
place one shape. **Rotation** turns it; **Direction / Velocity** drift it; **Fade Away** fades
it out over its life; **Hold Colour** keeps one palette colour per shape; **Fire Timing** fires a
new shape on every mark of a timing track instead of continuously. Buffer style and transform
are dials too: 81% of placements are `Per Preview` with `Rotate CC 90`, one file's way of
keeping its star upright on the tree.

Roles: **accent** (a one-beat white star, 350 placements), **ornament scatter** as a top layer
over a bed (hearts, snowflakes, presents, emoji on the whole house in the Christmas files), and
in one file a six-layer emoji row on the matrix. Shape sits on top: 90% of placements share the
model, and what is beneath is Spirals (1,049 pairs), Wave (259), Warp (111), Bars (64), Video,
all at Normal. Only another Shape, Faces, Pictures and one Curtain `1 is True Unmask` ever sit
above it. It is never a bed (median 0.35 s) and, in this corpus, never a mask:
`T_CHOICE_LayerMethod` is absent in 99% of placements. The Spirals file's "the Shape reveals the
twin" is the same Danger Zone placements seen from below; what xLights composites is a white star
at Normal popping over the twin, black-transparent everywhere else.

### Thematic fit

A shape is a *thing*, not a texture: a star is a star, a heart a heart, a present a present. It
says *ornament*, *burst*, *a rain of symbols*. The Danger Zone star is a flashbulb on the tree;
the Christmas hearts, snowflakes and presents are yard-wide confetti for a lyric (hearts on the
love line, presents on the gift line, snowflakes on the snow line). For Halloween the same move
is a scatter of orange Circles, a purple Star, or an Emoji by Char (pumpkin, ghost, bat):
Thickness 1 reads as a line-drawn symbol, Thickness 100 as a stamp. Shape is white in 75% of
placements (464) because the colour belongs to the bed beneath; when it carries colour it is one
colour per beat (red hearts, then green), not a palette: 89% of placements have one colour.

**How to permute.** Change the symbol with the lyric, not the section. Flip Rotation 212 ↔ 138 at
the phrase. Alternate the colour per beat (red hearts, green hearts). Move from Random Location (a
pop anywhere) to a placed Centre (the star at the top) for the hook. Trade the pop (Count 1,
StartSize 100) for a scatter (Count 41, StartSize 1, Growth 10) when the bed quietens. Change the
buffer between sections: Per Preview on the hero, Per Model Default on the yard, Model As A Pixel
when whole props should blink.

### Prop fit and render style

Mega tree 75% (2 seq — one file's star), matrix 15% (7 seq / 5 songs: the breadth), whole-house
groups 8% (6 seq / 5 songs), floods 2%. On a tree or matrix use `Per Preview` so the whole prop
is one canvas (the Danger Zone file adds `Rotate CC 90`). On the whole house use `Per Model
Default` or `Per Model Per Preview` so every prop gets its own scatter; `Single Line Model As A
Pixel` turns Count 22 circles into 22 props blinking. A shape on a line prop is a dot; on a
character or a stake at per-model it is a flash on that prop. For Rob's layout: the hero matrix
for stamps and the emoji row, SEM_ALL per model for the scatter.

### Timing

One beat (34%) and sub-beat (25%; 0.35 s median) for the pop, re-cut every beat; 1 beat per
colour for the hearts, 3 beats for the presents, 4 for the snowflakes; 5 beats for the drifting
star; 6 beats for the emoji row. Fades 0 on pops; 0.6–1 s out on the blobs; 0.75–1.5 in and
1–4 out on snowflakes.

### Anti-patterns

- **The black star.** 64 placements (Danger Zone 0:23–0:29) draw a `#000000` star at Normal,
  which is transparent: nothing renders. Mute a layer by removing it, not by colouring it black.
- **Shape held for a section.** Median 0.35 s; a Count-1 star left for 8 beats is one stamp.
- **Six colours.** 89% one colour; Hold Colour already gives each shape one, so a palette turns
  a scatter into confetti.
- **A scatter at Per Preview on the whole house.** Count 41 hearts across the yard land one per
  prop at best; use Per Model Default.
- **Random Location with Count 1 and a 4-beat hold.** The star picks one spot and sits.
- **Expecting a mask.** The blend defaults to Normal; a white star does not cut a window unless
  you set `1 is Mask` / `1 is Unmask`, and nobody in the corpus has.
- **Ours: Shape as filler on a group canvas.** It is a top layer; there has to be a bed.

### What code can realize today

- `look_id` + `knobs`: `Shape#15` (Rotation 138–212, Velocity 0–14) is the star pop; `#4`
  (CentreX/Y, Rotation 212–360, StartSize 30–100, Velocity 4–15, Hold Colour) and `#9`
  (Rotation 0–360 and its curves) the placed and spinning stars; `#5` / `#13` the rising outline;
  `#25` the heart scatter (no knobs); `#45` (Heart or Present, Count 59–100, Direction 90–268,
  Lifetime 23–41); `#48` snowflakes (Count 5 or 24, fades as knobs); `#26` (Circle, buffer `Per
  Model Per Preview` or `Single Line Model As A Pixel`, Count 22 or 5, Thickness 2–72); `#40` /
  `#39` the emoji; `#37` / `#38` fired stars. The pick_pool is `#15 #4 #5 #9 #26 #13`: five of
  six are the Danger Zone star, so an unnamed Shape layer is a white star unless you name a look.
- Fields: `cell_beats: 1` re-cuts the pop every beat; `palette` the colour; `blend`;
  `transform: "Rotate CC 90"` and `render_style: "Per Preview"` (both frozen into `#15`).
- `settings`: the curve strings above; `E_CHECKBOX_Shape_FireTiming=1` with
  `E_CHOICE_Shape_FireTimingTrack=Beats` after finalize.
- `effect_meta.py` gives Shape only an energy band (1–4): no speed key, no direction map, not
  cellable, so the weave never picks it; it is reachable only through a stack layer.
- Not synthesized: the six-layer staggered row (a layer has no start offset; six layers start
  together), the colour-per-beat alternation (one palette per layer; two layers with
  `cell_beats` cannot interleave), and any mask use.
