## Craft

### Identity and role

A continuous blended colour field that drifts and folds across the buffer. **Style** 1–7 picks
the pattern (1 in 57%, 4 seq / 4 songs; 6 and 7 one sequencer's, 26% and 17%); **Line
Density** is how many colour bands fold into the field (1 in 83%, 2 seq / 2 songs; 3 in 2 seq /
2 songs); **Speed** is how fast it drifts (85 / 87 / 91 one sequencer's, 80 in 2 seq / 2 songs,
69 once; 2 is the near-static version, one sequencer's chorus bed); **Color** is `Normal` in
100% (the palette; the presets never appear). **Blur** 9 (43%) or 15 (26%) or 6 (one
sequencer; absent in the other three). The palette is the real dial: black is a colour in 87%
(3 seq / 3 songs), four colours in 57%, six in 17%. Black is the hole: a plasma with black in the
palette has dark patches moving through it, which is what keeps it from being a wall. Sparkles
on 52% (one sequencer).

Roles: **bed**, and little else: 8–16 s 43%, ≥16 s 17%, one placement running the whole song
(Save Your Tears, 361 beats under a video). What sits above it: Spirals (5 pairs, 2 seq), Warp
(2), Pinwheel (2), SingleStrand, Faces, Video (1 each). A **reveal** once: `2 reveals 1` (9%,
2 placements, one sequencer) over a Fan pair, so the plasma shows only where the fans are lit
(the Fan file records the stack; from Plasma's side the miner did not pair it). A **twin** with
itself once (2 pairs: `Plasma · Plasma · Warp · Spirals · Butterfly`). Only 39% share the
model (3 seq / 3 songs): on arches, stakes and flakes it is usually alone. Never a carrier,
never an accent. Four sequences, four songs, and 18 of 23 placements from one of them.

### Thematic fit

It says *lava*, *aurora*, *slime*, *ectoplasm*, *nebula*, *oil on water*, and the palette
decides which. Eliot Wisser's `#FF80FF+#000000+#00FFFF+#8000FF` is a candy aurora (pink, cyan,
purple with a black hole); Sounding Joy's `#00FF00+#001200` is a green glow where the near-black
second colour keeps the field from going dark; Save Your Tears' `#0000FF+#000000+#00FFFF+#FF00FF`
is a blue nebula under a video. For Halloween, by palette only (not from the corpus): orange +
red + black Style 1 is **lava**; green + `#001200` is **slime**; purple + black + green on the
stakes is **ectoplasm** — and Eliot already put Style 6 at Speed 91 on his Pixel Stakes with a
`Blinds` in, which is Rob's tombstones. Christmas: the green chorus bed on the mini trees under
a Spirals twin. A song-long section (ambient, an intro, a video section) is where it earns the
whole canvas; everywhere else it is the floor under the feature.

**How to permute.** Change Style between props (1 on the arches, 6 on the stakes, 7 on the tree,
as Eliot did), not Speed, which lives at 80–91 unless you mean the near-static 2. Move the
black's position in the palette (`#000000+#FF80FF+#000000+#7F00FF` with two blacks is the
same sequencer's thinner field). Give the twin `Flip Horizontal`. Pick the entry per section
(`Blinds` on the stakes, `From Middle` on the arches). Put it under a different feature each
section: Spirals, then a Pinwheel under a Warp, then a Fan pair through the reveal.

What Plasma contributes: it covers. At Normal there are no gaps unless black is in the palette,
in which case the dark patches are transparent and the layer beneath shows through them as they
drift; without black a plasma on top hides everything. So it goes at the bottom, or on top only
with black and a purpose, or as the `2 reveals 1` layer where it paints the shape beneath.

### Prop fit and render style

Mega tree 48% (3 seq / 3 songs), arches 17%, stakes 13%, flakes 9%, a roofline, a mini-tree
group and a pixel field once (one sequencer each). A field wants pixels in two dimensions: the
tree and the matrix carry it as a picture. On arches `Per Model Per Preview` gives each arch its
own band of the fold; on stakes (columns) `Per Preview` runs one field across all of them so the
colour travels from stake to stake, which is the move for Rob's tombstones; `Horizontal Stack`
(Sounding Joy) lays the mini trees side by side as one wide field. Blur 9–15 softens the bands
and costs nothing on small props. On the Halloween layout: the hero matrix for lava or the
nebula under the feature, `Per Preview` slime across the stakes, nothing on the cats (a field
on a character is a colour wash with no shape).

### Anti-patterns

- **No black in the palette.** A solid wall of colour with no depth; 87% have a black or a
  near-black.
- **Plasma on top at Normal.** It covers the feature; put it beneath, or on top only with
  `2 reveals 1` or a black-heavy palette.
- **Speed 40–60.** Nobody; the corpus is 80–91, or 2, or 69 once.
- **A 2-beat plasma cell.** It is a bed; 9% and one habit.
- **Line Density 3 at Speed 91.** Not seen; 3 goes with Speed 2 or 69.
- **Our pipeline: the plasma on every quiet section.** It is `bed_capable` with energy 1–3 and a
  speed key 70–90 by intensity, so the fabric reaches for it as the bed of any soft section with
  the same look; it never sets Blur, so the bands are hard-edged on the hero matrix. Vary Style
  and palette per section and put `B_SLIDER_Blur` 9–15 in `settings`.
- **Six bright colours at Line Density 3.** Confetti; four with a black.
